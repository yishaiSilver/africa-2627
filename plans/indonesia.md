# Raja Ampat, West Papua: a week diving the world's richest reefs, a week in Waigeo's rainforest

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=indonesia

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $3,460 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** a cheaper, wilder alternative to the Africa trips, split evenly between reef and rainforest in Raja Ampat, West Papua.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 16 nights on the ground (plus 1 on a plane).
- **Route:** Jakarta 1n → Sorong 1n → **Arborek 7n** (10 dives, mantas, Piaynemo) → **Waigeo 6n** (birds-of-paradise, interior treks, night walks, Kali Biru) → Jakarta 1n.
- **Why Raja Ampat:** the Dampier Strait has the highest reef-fish and coral diversity recorded anywhere; Waigeo's interior is unlogged lowland rainforest with two birds-of-paradise found nowhere else. Few visitors, village homestays, no resorts needed.
- **Season:** October–April is the good diving season here. Expect warm showers and some wind; diving runs year-round in the strait.
- **Diving:** you're Advanced Open Water, so the Dampier Strait's current sites and deeper pinnacles (to 30 m) are open to you.
- **Budget: ~$3,460 pp**, under the $3.5k target, including ~5% contingency.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Jakarta (Los Angeles → in flight)

LAX → Jakarta (CGK) with one stop in Tokyo, Taipei or Seoul, ~22–24h. ANA showed ~$1,106 round trip for December 2026 dates.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Jakarta late (Jakarta)

Arrive CGK in the evening (the date line eats a day). Airport hotel; sleep.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Fly east to Papua (Jakarta → Sorong (West Papua))

Morning flight CGK → Sorong (~4h; Sorong is 2h ahead of Jakarta). Afternoon in Sorong: buy cash (IDR) here — the islands have no reliable ATMs.

*Overnight:* Sorong hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Ferry into Raja Ampat (Sorong → Waisai → Arborek)

09:00 fast ferry Sorong → Waisai (~2h). Pay the Raja Ampat marine park tag and visitor levy at Waisai. Homestay boat to Arborek (~1h). Afternoon: snorkel the Arborek jetty — schooling fish, often wobbegong sharks under the pier.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* L, D homestay

### Wed Dec 30 — Dive day 1: check-out and Arborek reefs (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives with Arborek Dive Shop (village-run): a check-out dive on the house reef, then a Dampier Strait site. Afternoon snorkel.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Thu Dec 31 — Dive day 2: Cape Kri and Sardine Reef (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on the strait's famous current sites — Cape Kri held the record for fish species counted on one dive. Expect strong currents; the guides pick the tide. New Year's Eve in the village.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Fri Jan 01 — Dive day 3: manta cleaning stations (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives at Manta Sandy / Manta Ridge, where reef mantas queue at cleaning stations, plus a reef dive. Snorkellers can watch from the surface on good days.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sat Jan 02 — Dive day 4: Blue Magic and Mioskon (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on pinnacles and reefs with big schools, sharks and pygmy seahorses (ask the guide to find them). Night snorkel off the jetty if conditions allow.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sun Jan 03 — Dive day 5: guides' choice (Dampier Strait (diving))

Last two dives of the package at the guides' pick for the conditions. Afternoon free in the water.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 04 — Karst islands and lagoons (Piaynemo / Fam islands)

Shared boat day trip west to Piaynemo: the climb to the viewpoint over the karst islands that's on every Raja Ampat poster, then snorkelling in the lagoons on the way back. A no-dive day before the forest.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Tue Jan 05 — To the rainforest (Arborek → Waigeo (Saporkren))

Homestay boat to Waigeo, Raja Ampat's biggest island (~1.5h). Saporkren is a village between the reef and primary lowland rainforest. Afternoon walk on the forest edge with a village guide.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Wed Jan 06 — Birds-of-paradise at dawn (Waigeo rainforest)

Pre-dawn steep ~40-min climb to the red bird-of-paradise display tree: males dance in the canopy at first light. Afternoon by 4WD and on foot to a hide for Wilson's bird-of-paradise, found only on Waigeo and Batanta.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Thu Jan 07 — Deep into the interior (Waigeo rainforest)

Full day on foot into unlogged lowland forest with a village guide: giant buttressed trees, hornbills crashing overhead, cockatoos and parrots, monitor lizards. After dark, a night walk for cuscus (tree-dwelling marsupials), frogs and geckos.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Fri Jan 08 — The blue river (Waigeo (Kali Biru))

Drive to Warsambin and trek ~1–1.5h through the forest to Kali Biru, a river of startling clear blue water in the jungle — swim in it. Forest birding on the walk back.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Sat Jan 09 — Mangroves, reef and a second night walk (Waigeo coast)

Morning snorkel off Saporkren's own reef and a paddle or boat trip into the mangroves. Rest through the midday heat. Second night walk on a different trail.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sun Jan 10 — Last dawn in the forest (Waigeo rainforest)

Back to the display trees at dawn (better photos the second time), then a long forest hike on a new trail with your guide. Pack in the evening.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 11 — Back to Jakarta (Waigeo → Sorong → Jakarta)

Car Saporkren → Waisai (~30m), 09:00 ferry to Sorong (~2h), afternoon flight Sorong → Jakarta (~4h). Airport hotel.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Fly home (Jakarta → Los Angeles)

CGK → LAX with one stop; the date line gives the day back, so you land the same calendar day. Leave 24h between your last dive (Jan 3) and flying — easily met.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **6 more dives** (+$195 pp): Swap the Piaynemo day and an afternoon for dives (~IDR 3.45M package).
- **Arfak Mountains instead of 4 Waigeo nights** (+$400 pp): Montane rainforest above Manokwari: western parotia, Vogelkop bowerbird, magnificent bird-of-paradise from village hides. Adds a Sorong → Manokwari flight and 4WD road. Rough estimate — local rates unconfirmed.
- **Add 3 days of orangutans in Tanjung Puting (Borneo)** (+$650 pp): Klotok houseboat from Pangkalan Bun (~$395 pp for two) plus two domestic flights and a Jakarta night. Trip 3 days longer.
- **Skip the Piaynemo day** (−$60 pp): Snorkel and rest at Arborek instead.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Indonesia:** e-Visa on Arrival, IDR 500k (~$32), 30 days. Official site only: evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- **Raja Ampat:** marine park tag (IDR 700k) + visitor levy (IDR 1M) ≈ IDR 1.7M, cash at Waisai. Carry the tag.

## Money
- **Cash is everything.** Homestays, dive shops, boats and guides want IDR cash. Withdraw in Sorong; Waisai's ATMs are unreliable and the islands have none.

## Packing
- **No camping gear needed.** Homestays are simple huts with a mattress and mosquito net, shared bathrooms (often a bucket 'mandi'), generator power in the evening.
- Your own mask, snorkel, fins and dive computer; a rash guard; reef-safe sunscreen; a dry bag.
- Binoculars for the birds-of-paradise; a headtorch for night walks; light long sleeves and trousers against mosquitoes; trail shoes that can get wet.
- Domestic flights allow ~20 kg checked; keep dive gear light.

## Health and safety
- **Malaria:** CDC lists all of West Papua (incl. Raja Ampat) as a risk area — take prophylaxis (atovaquone-proguanil). Dengue too: DEET or picaridin day and night.
- **Diving:** strong currents in the Dampier Strait; dive with the local guides and a surface marker. Get dive accident cover — the nearest recompression chamber may be hours away.
- Medical care on the islands is minimal; evacuation cover is essential.
- Re-check the State Department's Indonesia advisory (it flags parts of the Papuan highlands, not Raja Ampat) before booking.

## Season
- Raja Ampat's diving season is roughly October–April; December–January brings showers and some northwest wind, but the Dampier Strait stays diveable.
