# Borneo, North Sulawesi & Raja Ampat: orangutans by houseboat, tarsiers, muck diving, the world's richest reefs and Waigeo at night

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=indonesia

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-16 · **Planned cost:** $4,370 per person

## Overview

- **Built around reptiles, amphibians, mammals and fish** (birds are a bonus): apes and monkeys in Borneo, primates and marsupials in Sulawesi, critters and big reef fish underwater, night walks for cuscus, frogs and snakes on Waigeo.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Sat Jan 16. 20 nights on the ground (plus 1 on a plane).
- **Route:** Jakarta 1n → **Tanjung Puting 3n** (houseboat) → Jakarta 1n → **Tangkoko 3n** → **Lembeh 1n** (3 muck dives) → ✈ direct to Sorong 1n → **Arborek 6n** (10 dives, mantas) → **Waigeo 3n** (treks, two night walks, Kali Biru) → Jakarta 1n.
- **Borneo:** four days on a private klotok up the Sekonyer River: orangutans at Camp Leakey, a full day trekking deeper into the forest, proboscis monkeys roosting along the river at dusk, gibbons calling, crocodiles by spotlight.
- **Two kinds of diving:** One day at Lembeh, the world capital of muck diving: frogfish, mimic octopus, flamboyant cuttlefish, mandarinfish. The Dampier Strait has the highest reef-fish and coral diversity recorded anywhere (mantas, sharks, big schools). You're Advanced Open Water, so the current sites and deeper pinnacles are open to you.
- **To fit Jan 16,** the Piaynemo viewpoint day was dropped, and Lembeh trimmed to one dive day (13 dives in total). The freed days went to Borneo and a third Tangkoko night.
- **Budget: ~$4,370 pp**, including ~5% contingency.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Jakarta (Los Angeles → in flight)

LAX → Jakarta (CGK) with one stop in Tokyo, Taipei or Seoul, ~22–24h. ANA showed ~$1,106 round trip for December 2026 dates.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Jakarta late (Jakarta)

Arrive CGK in the evening (the date line eats a day). Airport hotel; sleep.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Borneo: up the Sekonyer River by houseboat (Jakarta → Pangkalan Bun → Tanjung Puting)

Early flight CGK → Pangkalan Bun (~1h20). The crew meets you and drives you 20 minutes to Kumai harbour, where you board a private klotok, a wooden houseboat with a crew, guide and cook. Cruise up the Sekonyer River into Tanjung Puting National Park, past long-tailed macaques and kingfishers. Afternoon at the Tanjung Harapan feeding station for orangutans. At dusk, proboscis monkeys settle into the riverside trees to sleep. You sleep on the top deck on mattresses under mosquito nets (no tent needed).

*Overnight:* Klotok houseboat, Tanjung Puting · *Meals:* L, D on board

### Tue Dec 29 — Orangutans at Camp Leakey (Tanjung Puting National Park)

Morning at Pondok Tanggui, then upriver to Camp Leakey, the research camp Biruté Galdikas founded in 1971. Orangutans at the feeding platform and around camp, plus bearded pigs and gibbons calling in the forest. Afternoon forest trek with your guide. After dark, spotlight the river from the boat for crocodiles (the rare false gharial lives here) and watch fireflies in the riverside trees.

*Overnight:* Klotok houseboat, Tanjung Puting · *Meals:* B, L, D on board

### Wed Dec 30 — A full day deeper in the forest (Tanjung Puting National Park)

Zero transfer. A longer guided trek from Camp Leakey into the peat-swamp and heath forest, where you're more likely to meet wild (not just feeding-station) orangutans, gibbons and pig-tailed macaques. Back on the river at dusk for proboscis monkeys. Ask your operator about a guided night walk; some boats stop at the Pondok Ambung research station for one (frogs, spiders, sleeping birds; tarsiers and slow lorises are rare).

*Overnight:* Klotok houseboat, Tanjung Puting · *Meals:* B, L, D on board

### Thu Dec 31 — Last forest walk, back to Jakarta (Tanjung Puting → Pangkalan Bun → Jakarta)

Dawn on the river: proboscis monkeys and hornbills. A short jungle walk, then downriver to Kumai by midday. Afternoon flight Pangkalan Bun → Jakarta. Airport hotel. New Year's Eve at the airport hotel.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* B, L on board; D own

### Fri Jan 01 — To North Sulawesi; tarsiers at dusk (Jakarta → Manado → Tangkoko)

Morning flight CGK → Manado (~3.5h; Manado is 1h ahead). Car to Batuputih at the edge of Tangkoko reserve (~2h). At dusk, a ranger takes you to a strangler fig where a family of spectral tarsiers — fist-sized primates with enormous eyes — wake up and leap off to hunt insects.

*Overnight:* Batuputih homestay, Tangkoko (full board) · *Meals:* D homestay

### Sat Jan 02 — Macaques, cuscus and a night walk (Tangkoko Nature Reserve)

Dawn walk with a ranger: troops of crested black macaques (critically endangered, unafraid of people) foraging on the forest floor, bear cuscus and Sulawesi dwarf cuscus in the canopy, flying lizards gliding between trunks. Rest through midday. Night walk: hunting tarsiers, Sulawesi pit vipers, frogs, sleeping birds and civets if lucky.

*Overnight:* Batuputih homestay, Tangkoko (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sun Jan 03 — Third Tangkoko day: second chances (Tangkoko Nature Reserve)

Dawn walk with the ranger for whatever you missed, often the dwarf cuscus or the bear cuscus in better light. Late afternoon along the Batuputih shore and mangroves for monitor lizards, kingfishers and hornbills. Third night walk on a different trail: pit vipers, frogs, hunting tarsiers.

*Overnight:* Batuputih homestay, Tangkoko (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 04 — One day of muck diving (Tangkoko → Lembeh Strait)

Car to Bitung (~1.5h) after breakfast and boat to a Lembeh dive resort by late morning. Two afternoon muck dives on black volcanic sand (frogfish, mimic and wonderpus octopus, flamboyant cuttlefish, ghost pipefish, pygmy seahorses), then the dusk dive for mating mandarinfish.

*Overnight:* Budget dive resort, Lembeh Strait (full board) · *Meals:* L, D resort

### Tue Jan 05 — Fly to West Papua (Lembeh → Manado → Sorong)

Slow morning, no diving: you need ~18h between the dusk dive and the flight. Car to Manado airport (~1.5h). Afternoon direct flight Manado → Sorong (~1h25; Sorong is 1h ahead). Book one leaving after ~13:00. Buy all the cash (IDR) you'll need in Raja Ampat in Sorong; the islands have no reliable ATMs.

*Overnight:* Sorong hotel · *Meals:* B resort; L, D own

### Wed Jan 06 — Ferry into Raja Ampat (Sorong → Waisai → Arborek)

09:00 fast ferry Sorong → Waisai (~2h). Pay the Raja Ampat marine park tag and visitor levy at Waisai. Homestay boat to Arborek (~1h). Snorkel the Arborek jetty — schooling fish, wobbegong sharks under the pier — and look after dark for Raja Ampat's endemic 'walking' epaulette shark in the shallows.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* L, D homestay

### Thu Jan 07 — Dive day 1: check-out and Arborek reefs (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives with Arborek Dive Shop (village-run): a check-out dive, then a Dampier Strait site. Afternoon snorkel.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Fri Jan 08 — Dive day 2: Cape Kri and Sardine Reef (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on the strait's famous current sites — Cape Kri held the record for fish species counted on one dive. Strong currents; the guides pick the tide.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sat Jan 09 — Dive day 3: manta cleaning stations (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives at Manta Sandy / Manta Ridge, where reef mantas queue at cleaning stations, plus a reef dive.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sun Jan 10 — Dive day 4: Blue Magic and Mioskon (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on pinnacles with big schools, sharks and pygmy seahorses — deeper sites your Advanced Open Water covers. Night snorkel off the jetty.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 11 — Dive day 5: guides' choice (Dampier Strait (diving))

Last two dives of the package at the guides' pick for the conditions.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Tue Jan 12 — To Waigeo's rainforest; first night walk (Arborek → Waigeo (Saporkren))

Homestay boat to Waigeo, Raja Ampat's biggest island (~1.5h). Afternoon forest walk with a village guide; monitor lizards on the forest edge. After dark, a night walk for the Waigeo spotted cuscus (found only on this island), northern common cuscus, fruit bats, frogs and snakes.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Wed Jan 13 — The blue river (Waigeo (Kali Biru))

Drive to Warsambin and trek ~1–1.5h through primary forest to Kali Biru, a river of clear blue water in the jungle — swim in it, look for skinks, frogs and freshwater fish. Second night walk on a different trail.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Thu Jan 14 — Deep into the interior (Waigeo rainforest)

Full day on foot into unlogged lowland forest with a village guide: giant buttressed trees, lizards and frogs along the streams, hornbills and cockatoos overhead. Optional: the dawn red bird-of-paradise display before you set off.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Fri Jan 15 — Back to Jakarta (Waigeo → Sorong → Jakarta)

Car Saporkren → Waisai (~30m), 09:00 ferry to Sorong (~2h), afternoon flight Sorong → Jakarta (~4h). Airport hotel.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 16 — Fly home (Jakarta → Los Angeles)

CGK → LAX with one stop; the date line gives the day back, so you land the same calendar day. Last dive was Jan 11.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Practical

## Entry
- **Indonesia:** e-Visa on Arrival, IDR 500k (~$32), 30 days. Official site only: evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- **Raja Ampat:** marine park tag (IDR 700k) + visitor levy (IDR 1M) ≈ IDR 1.7M, cash at Waisai. Carry the tag.
- **Lembeh:** small park entry (~IDR 100k) in cash.

## Money
- **Cash is everything.** Homestays, dive shops, boats and guides want IDR cash. Manado and Sorong have ATMs; Waisai's are unreliable and the islands have none.

## Packing
- **No camping gear needed.** Homestays are simple huts with a mattress and mosquito net, shared bathrooms (often a bucket 'mandi'), generator power in the evening.
- Your own mask, snorkel, fins and dive computer; a rash guard; reef-safe sunscreen; a dry bag.
- A strong headtorch (plus a red-light mode) for night walks and spotting eyeshine; binoculars; light long sleeves and trousers against mosquitoes; trail shoes that can get wet.
- Domestic flights allow ~20 kg checked; keep dive gear light.

## Health and safety
- **Malaria:** CDC lists all of West Papua (incl. Raja Ampat) as a risk area, and rural Sulawesi too — take prophylaxis (atovaquone-proguanil). Dengue too: DEET or picaridin day and night.
- **Diving:** strong currents in the Dampier Strait; dive with the local guides and a surface marker. Get dive accident cover — the nearest recompression chamber may be hours away.
- Medical care on the islands is minimal; evacuation cover is essential.
- Re-check the State Department's Indonesia advisory (it flags parts of the Papuan highlands, not Raja Ampat) before booking.

## Season
- Raja Ampat's diving season is roughly October–April; December–January brings showers and some northwest wind, but the Dampier Strait stays diveable.
- North Sulawesi is in its wet season (Dec–Jan): expect rain at Tangkoko; Lembeh diving runs year-round.
- Flying after diving: the dusk dive on Jan 4 ends ~18:30, so the Jan 5 Manado → Sorong flight should leave after ~13:00 (18h+). If only a morning flight runs, skip the dusk dive. The last Raja Ampat dive (Jan 11) is 4 days before flying.
- Central Kalimantan is wet in Dec–Jan too; the klotok has a roof, and the river trips run year-round.
