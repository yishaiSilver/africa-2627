# North Sulawesi & Raja Ampat: tarsiers, muck diving, the world's richest reefs and Waigeo's rainforest at night

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=indonesia

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-14 · **Planned cost:** $3,892 per person

## Overview

- **Built around reptiles, amphibians, mammals and fish** (birds are a bonus): primates and marsupials in Sulawesi, critters and big reef fish underwater, night walks for cuscus, frogs and snakes on Waigeo.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Thu Jan 14. 18 nights on the ground (plus 1 on a plane).
- **Route:** Jakarta 1n → **Tangkoko 2n** → **Lembeh Strait 3n** (6 muck dives) → ✈ direct to Sorong 1n → **Arborek 7n** (10 dives, mantas, Piaynemo) → **Waigeo 3n** (treks, two night walks, Kali Biru) → Jakarta 1n.
- **Two kinds of diving:** Lembeh is the world capital of muck diving — frogfish, mimic octopus, flamboyant cuttlefish, mandarinfish. The Dampier Strait has the highest reef-fish and coral diversity recorded anywhere — mantas, sharks, big schools. You're Advanced Open Water, so the current sites and deeper pinnacles are open to you.
- **Mammals on land:** spectral tarsiers and crested black macaques at Tangkoko (both near-certain), bear and dwarf cuscus there, and the Waigeo spotted cuscus — found only on Waigeo — on night walks.
- **Season:** October–April is the good diving season in Raja Ampat; North Sulawesi is wet in Dec–Jan but Lembeh diving runs year-round.
- **Budget: ~$3,892 pp**, about $430 more than the reef + Waigeo-only version, including ~5% contingency.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Jakarta (Los Angeles → in flight)

LAX → Jakarta (CGK) with one stop in Tokyo, Taipei or Seoul, ~22–24h. ANA showed ~$1,106 round trip for December 2026 dates.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Jakarta late (Jakarta)

Arrive CGK in the evening (the date line eats a day). Airport hotel; sleep.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — To North Sulawesi; tarsiers at dusk (Jakarta → Manado → Tangkoko)

Morning flight CGK → Manado (~3.5h; Manado is 1h ahead). Car to Batuputih at the edge of Tangkoko reserve (~2h). At dusk, a ranger takes you to a strangler fig where a family of spectral tarsiers — fist-sized primates with enormous eyes — wake up and leap off to hunt insects.

*Overnight:* Batuputih homestay, Tangkoko (full board) · *Meals:* D homestay

### Tue Dec 29 — Macaques, cuscus and a night walk (Tangkoko Nature Reserve)

Dawn walk with a ranger: troops of crested black macaques (critically endangered, unafraid of people) foraging on the forest floor, bear cuscus and Sulawesi dwarf cuscus in the canopy, flying lizards gliding between trunks. Rest through midday. Night walk: hunting tarsiers, Sulawesi pit vipers, frogs, sleeping birds and civets if lucky.

*Overnight:* Batuputih homestay, Tangkoko (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Wed Dec 30 — Second dawn walk, then into the muck (Tangkoko → Lembeh Strait)

Second dawn walk for anything missed. Car to Bitung (~1.5h) and boat to a Lembeh dive resort. One afternoon dive to settle in on black volcanic sand.

*Overnight:* Budget dive resort, Lembeh Strait (full board) · *Meals:* B; L, D resort

### Thu Dec 31 — Muck diving: three dives (Lembeh Strait (diving))

Two day dives on Lembeh's black sand and rubble — frogfish, mimic and wonderpus octopus, flamboyant cuttlefish, ghost pipefish, pygmy seahorses, nudibranchs by the dozen — and a dusk dive for mating mandarinfish. New Year's Eve at the resort.

*Overnight:* Budget dive resort, Lembeh Strait (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D resort

### Fri Jan 01 — Two last muck dives (Lembeh Strait (diving))

Two morning dives, then out of the water by midday to leave ~22h before tomorrow's flight. Afternoon off.

*Overnight:* Budget dive resort, Lembeh Strait (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D resort

### Sat Jan 02 — Fly to West Papua (Lembeh → Manado → Sorong)

Car to Manado airport (~1.5h). Direct flight Manado → Sorong (~1h25; Sorong is 1h ahead again). Buy all the cash (IDR) you'll need in Raja Ampat here — the islands have no reliable ATMs.

*Overnight:* Sorong hotel · *Meals:* B resort; L, D own

### Sun Jan 03 — Ferry into Raja Ampat (Sorong → Waisai → Arborek)

09:00 fast ferry Sorong → Waisai (~2h). Pay the Raja Ampat marine park tag and visitor levy at Waisai. Homestay boat to Arborek (~1h). Snorkel the Arborek jetty — schooling fish, wobbegong sharks under the pier — and look after dark for Raja Ampat's endemic 'walking' epaulette shark in the shallows.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 04 — Dive day 1: check-out and Arborek reefs (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives with Arborek Dive Shop (village-run): a check-out dive, then a Dampier Strait site. Afternoon snorkel.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Tue Jan 05 — Dive day 2: Cape Kri and Sardine Reef (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on the strait's famous current sites — Cape Kri held the record for fish species counted on one dive. Strong currents; the guides pick the tide.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Wed Jan 06 — Dive day 3: manta cleaning stations (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives at Manta Sandy / Manta Ridge, where reef mantas queue at cleaning stations, plus a reef dive.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Thu Jan 07 — Dive day 4: Blue Magic and Mioskon (Dampier Strait (diving))

Two dives on pinnacles with big schools, sharks and pygmy seahorses — deeper sites your Advanced Open Water covers. Night snorkel off the jetty.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Fri Jan 08 — Dive day 5: guides' choice (Dampier Strait (diving))

Last two dives of the package at the guides' pick for the conditions.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Sat Jan 09 — Karst islands and lagoons (Piaynemo / Fam islands)

Shared boat day trip west to Piaynemo: the climb to the viewpoint over the karst islands, then snorkelling in the lagoons on the way back.

*Overnight:* Arborek village homestay (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Sun Jan 10 — To Waigeo's rainforest; first night walk (Arborek → Waigeo (Saporkren))

Homestay boat to Waigeo, Raja Ampat's biggest island (~1.5h). Afternoon forest walk with a village guide; monitor lizards on the forest edge. After dark, a night walk for the Waigeo spotted cuscus (found only on this island), northern common cuscus, fruit bats, frogs and snakes.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D homestay

### Mon Jan 11 — The blue river (Waigeo (Kali Biru))

Drive to Warsambin and trek ~1–1.5h through primary forest to Kali Biru, a river of clear blue water in the jungle — swim in it, look for skinks, frogs and freshwater fish. Second night walk on a different trail.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Tue Jan 12 — Deep into the interior (Waigeo rainforest)

Full day on foot into unlogged lowland forest with a village guide: giant buttressed trees, lizards and frogs along the streams, hornbills and cockatoos overhead. Optional: the dawn red bird-of-paradise display before you set off.

*Overnight:* Saporkren homestay, Waigeo (full board) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D homestay

### Wed Jan 13 — Back to Jakarta (Waigeo → Sorong → Jakarta)

Car Saporkren → Waisai (~30m), 09:00 ferry to Sorong (~2h), afternoon flight Sorong → Jakarta (~4h). Airport hotel.

*Overnight:* Jakarta airport hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Thu Jan 14 — Fly home (Jakarta → Los Angeles)

CGK → LAX with one stop; the date line gives the day back, so you land the same calendar day. Last dive was Jan 8.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **3 more Lembeh dives** (+$170 pp): Add a fourth Lembeh night with a full dive day (incl. a night dive for bobtail squid and octopus).
- **6 more Raja Ampat dives** (+$195 pp): Swap the Piaynemo day and an afternoon for dives (~IDR 3.45M package).
- **Dawn red bird-of-paradise on Waigeo** (+$70 pp): The steep ~40-min climb to the display tree with a local birding guide.
- **Add 3 days of orangutans in Tanjung Puting (Borneo)** (+$650 pp): Klotok houseboat from Pangkalan Bun (~$395 pp for two): orangutans, proboscis monkeys, gibbons, crocodiles along the river. Two extra domestic flights; trip 3 days longer.
- **Skip the Piaynemo day** (−$60 pp): Snorkel and rest at Arborek instead.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Indonesia:** e-Visa on Arrival, IDR 500k (~$32), 30 days. Official site only: evisa.imigrasi.go.id. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- **Raja Ampat:** marine park tag (IDR 700k) + visitor levy (IDR 1M) ≈ IDR 1.7M, cash at Waisai. Carry the tag.
- **Lembeh:** small park entry (~IDR 100k) in cash.

## Money
- **Cash is everything.** Homestays, dive shops, boats and guides want IDR cash. Manado and Sorong have ATMs; Waisai's are unreliable and the islands have none.

## Packing
- **No camping gear needed.** Homestays are simple huts with a mattress and mosquito net, shared bathrooms (often a bucket 'mandi'), generator power in the evening.
- Your own mask, snorkel, fins and dive computer; a rash guard; reef-safe sunscreen; a dry bag.
- A strong headtorch (plus a red-light mode) for night walks and spotting eyeshine; binoculars; light long sleeves and trousers against mosquitoes; trail shoes that can get wet.
- Domestic flights allow ~20 kg checked; keep dive gear light.

## Health and safety
- **Malaria:** CDC lists all of West Papua (incl. Raja Ampat) as a risk area, and rural Sulawesi too — take prophylaxis (atovaquone-proguanil). Dengue too: DEET or picaridin day and night.
- **Diving:** strong currents in the Dampier Strait; dive with the local guides and a surface marker. Get dive accident cover — the nearest recompression chamber may be hours away.
- Medical care on the islands is minimal; evacuation cover is essential.
- Re-check the State Department's Indonesia advisory (it flags parts of the Papuan highlands, not Raja Ampat) before booking.

## Season
- Raja Ampat's diving season is roughly October–April; December–January brings showers and some northwest wind, but the Dampier Strait stays diveable.
- North Sulawesi is in its wet season (Dec–Jan): expect rain at Tangkoko; Lembeh diving runs year-round.
- Flying after diving: the plan leaves ~22h after the last Lembeh dive and 5 days after the last Raja Ampat dive.
