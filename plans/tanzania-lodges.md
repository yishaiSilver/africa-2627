# Istanbul & Tanzania without camping: Serengeti tented camp, Karatu, Mikumi and the Udzungwa rainforest

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=tanzania-lodges

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $6,290 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** the Tanzania trip ($5,820) with **no camping**. Every night has a real bed and an en-suite bathroom. Same dates, same parks, same Mikumi + Udzungwa tour.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 17 nights: 3 Istanbul, 13 Tanzania, 1 on a plane.
- **Route:** Istanbul 3n → Arusha 1n → **6-day private lodge safari** (Tarangire → Mto wa Mbu lodge 1n, Serengeti tented camp 3n, Karatu lodge 1n, crater) → Arusha 1n → fly to Dar → **4-day Mikumi + Udzungwa** → home from Dar.
- **No rim night:** Simba campsite (2,300 m, near freezing) becomes a lodge in Karatu, ~45 min below the crater gate at ~1,500 m. You still descend into the crater at opening; the start is 05:30 instead of 06:00.
- **Serengeti:** a budget permanent tented camp (e.g. Embalakai or Tukaone): canvas walls, but real beds, en-suite bathroom, full board. Nothing cheaper sits inside the park, and staying outside means 1h+ each way.
- **Night drive moves to Mikumi:** Tarangire's night drive needs you inside the park after dark, and the only in-park lodge is ~$300 pp. Mikumi is also on TANAPA's night-drive list, so it goes there (Jan 9). The Tarangire ranger walk stays.
- **Budget: ~$6,290 pp, +$470 vs the camping version**, still with **no contingency**. The safari line is the whole difference (+$500, camps at ~$185–260 pppn plus the $71 Serengeti concession fee vs ~$35 campsites); no cook means ~$30 less in tips. The quote range runs to $3,000, which would put the trip ~$300 over the +$500 line.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

TK10 LAX 19:45 → IST 19:00 next day (~13h). Istanbul is on Tanzania time (UTC+3), so the jet-lag shift is done once, here.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Istanbul, metro into the city (Istanbul)

Land 19:00. Metro M11 → M2 → T1 to Sultanahmet, ~1.5h door to door. Buy an Istanbulkart at the station. Easy dinner, early night.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Sultanahmet on foot (Istanbul)

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern — all within ten minutes of each other. Zero travel.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus (Istanbul)

Topkapı in the morning, Grand Bazaar after lunch, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for dinner.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Free morning, fly to Kilimanjaro (Istanbul → in flight)

Spice Bazaar or Süleymaniye. Bags at the hotel. Leave for the airport by 15:00. TK Istanbul 18:30 → Kilimanjaro 01:40 (summer 2026 timing; confirm the Dec schedule).

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B; in-flight

### Thu Dec 31 — Land 01:40, rest day in Arusha (Kilimanjaro → Arusha)

Visa check at JRO, pre-booked taxi to Arusha (~1h). Room held from Dec 30 so it's ready at 03:00. Sleep in, then a slow day: Tanzanian SIM, cash, meet the safari operator for the briefing. Quiet New Year's Eve.

*Overnight:* Arusha budget hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Fri Jan 01 — Safari day 1: Tarangire on foot (Arusha → Tarangire → Mto wa Mbu)

Pickup ~07:00, ~2.5h to Tarangire. Late-morning drive: big elephant herds, baobabs, lion. Mid-afternoon, a 2–3h walking safari with an armed TANAPA ranger, on foot among elephant, giraffe and zebra. Last drive until the 18:00 close, then ~1h to a lodge in Mto wa Mbu, on the way to the Serengeti.

*Overnight:* Budget lodge, Mto wa Mbu (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Sat Jan 02 — Safari day 2: into the Serengeti (Ngorongoro highlands → Serengeti)

Up the Rift escarpment and over the Ngorongoro highlands (rim forest views), down through the conservation area to Naabi Hill gate, game drive to camp. ~5–6h with driving and drives, an hour shorter than from Tarangire. In January the herds are on the southern short-grass plains, so you may cross them on the way in.

*Overnight:* Budget tented camp, central Serengeti (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Sun Jan 03 — Safari day 3: leopard day (Serengeti (Seronera))

Zero transfer. Dawn to dusk in the Seronera valley: the sausage trees and kopjes are the best leopard country on the circuit. Lion and cheetah are routine here. Camps like Embalakai and Tukaone are 40–60 min from Seronera, so leave at first light.

*Overnight:* Budget tented camp, central Serengeti (in package) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D safari

### Mon Jan 04 — Safari day 4: the herds or more cats (Serengeti)

Zero transfer. Ask the guide on day 2 whether a run toward the southern plains / Ndutu edge is worth it this year; otherwise a second full Seronera day for leopard.

*Overnight:* Budget tented camp, central Serengeti (in package) · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D safari

### Tue Jan 05 — Safari day 5: last drive, down to Karatu (Serengeti → Karatu)

Morning drive, then out via Naabi Hill gate in the early afternoon and across the conservation area to a lodge in Karatu (~4h). Time the Ngorongoro entry: the 24h permit starts when you enter at Naabi and must still be valid when you leave the crater tomorrow, so don't enter before ~13:00. Karatu sits at ~1,500 m: cool, not freezing. Hot shower, real bed.

*Overnight:* Budget lodge, Karatu (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Wed Jan 06 — Safari day 6: crater floor for rhino (Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha)

Leave Karatu 05:30, ~45 min up to Lodoare gate and the descent road at opening. Full 6h crater allowance: black rhino (the best chance on the trip), lion, hyena, flamingo. Out of the conservation area by ~13:00 (inside the 24h permit), ~3h back to Arusha.

*Overnight:* Arusha budget hotel · *Meals:* B, L safari; D own

### Thu Jan 07 — Fly south to Dar (Arusha → Dar es Salaam)

Precision Air or Air Tanzania, Arusha (ARK) or Kilimanjaro (JRO) → Dar, ~1h15 direct. Afternoon off; laundry. Meet the Mikumi–Udzungwa operator.

*Overnight:* Dar es Salaam budget hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Fri Jan 08 — South trip day 1: Mikumi (Dar → Mikumi)

Operator pickup. SGR train to Morogoro (~1.5h) then road to Mikumi, or ~5h by road. Afternoon game drive: elephant, giraffe, buffalo, hippo pools, lion.

*Overnight:* Lodge/camp near Mikumi (in package) · *Meals:* L, D tour

### Sat Jan 09 — South trip day 2: full day in Mikumi, night drive (Mikumi NP)

Zero transfer. Full-day drive on the Mkata floodplain; leopard is possible in the wooded hills. Few vehicles compared with the north. After dinner, a night drive with a spotlight: bushbaby, genet, civet, porcupine, hyena. Option: a ranger-led morning walk too.

*Overnight:* Lodge/camp near Mikumi (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D tour

### Sun Jan 10 — South trip day 3: rainforest on foot (Udzungwa Mountains NP)

~1.5h to Mang'ula. Full day hiking with a park guide: Sanje Falls trail (170 m falls, ~5–6h), with a primate-focused walk for the Udzungwa (Iringa) red colobus and Sanje crested mangabey. Chameleons, forest birds. Udzungwa is walking-only — no vehicles inside.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse/camp at Mang'ula (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D tour

### Mon Jan 11 — Back to Dar (Udzungwa → Dar es Salaam)

Early start, road to Morogoro (~3h) and SGR or road back to Dar. Hotel near the airport with the room until midnight; shower, repack, sleep a few hours.

*Overnight:* Dar hotel near the airport (until ~midnight) · *Meals:* B tour; L, D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Fly home (Dar es Salaam → Los Angeles)

Leave the hotel ~00:30. TK604 DAR ~03:25 → IST ~11:05, then TK9 → LAX, arriving the same evening. About 27h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Camp one night in Tarangire for its night drive** (+$60 pp): The one camping night that isn't cold: Tarangire is at ~1,100 m. Public campsite instead of Mto wa Mbu, and the Tarangire night drive on top of Mikumi's. Day 2 goes back to ~6–7h.
- **7-day safari: add a Lake Manyara day** (+$300 pp): Groundwater forest with blue monkeys, baboons and elephants on the way back, plus another lodge night. Puts the trip well past +$500.
- **Stay in Istanbul until Dec 31** (+$95 pp): A fourth night (paid) and another day of food. Fare difference unknown until you price it; flying on Dec 31 is sometimes cheaper.
- **Second Udzungwa day** (+$200 pp): The red colobus and mangabey trails are different from the Sanje Falls route. One more lodge night, guide and park fee.
- **Arusha National Park day** (+$180 pp): Forest with black-and-white colobus, buffalo, giraffe, and walking safaris with an armed ranger. Fits on Dec 31 only if you skip the rest day.
- **Buffer day in Dar before the flight** (+$55 pp): Avoids the Udzungwa → Dar → 03:25 flight in one stretch. Home Jan 13.
- **Skip the Topkapı Harem ticket** (−$25 pp): 
- **Mikumi ranger-led morning walk (Jan 9)** (+$35 pp): ~$20–25 walking fee + ranger, ask the tour operator. The night drive is already in the plan.
- **Shared group lodge safari instead of private** (−$450 pp): A fixed-route group lodge safari. Drops the Tarangire walk, which a shared group can't add; the Mikumi night drive is unaffected.
- **Comprehensive insurance with trip cancellation** (+$110 pp): Instead of medical + evacuation only.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Tanzania:** US citizens need the $100 multiple-entry visa. Apply only at visa.immigration.go.tz; lookalike sites overcharge. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- **Yellow fever:** not required arriving from Istanbul. Tanzania asks for it only after >12h transit in a risk country (Kenya counts — don't route via Nairobi).
- Türkiye is visa-free for US tourists.

## Packing
- One soft bag each; the Arusha → Dar flight has tight bag limits.
- **No camping gear needed.** Every night is a room or a permanent tent with a bed and bathroom.
- **Warm layers for dawn drives** and the 06:00 crater descent (the rim road is at 2,300 m). No cold nights: Karatu is ~1,500 m. Istanbul is 4–9°C and wet.
- Hiking shoes with grip for the Sanje Falls trail; it's steep and muddy in places. Headtorch for the night drive and lodge paths.
- Neutral colours; tsetse flies in Tarangire and parts of the Serengeti go for dark blue and black.
- Plastic bags are banned in Tanzania, including in luggage.

## Money
- Park fees are cashless and paid by the operator. Carry crisp post-2013 USD for tips and the visa backup; TZS for meals and taxis.

## Season
- Early January is the short dry spell between rains. Wildebeest herds are on the southern Serengeti / Ndutu plains, with calving from late January.

## Health
- Malaria prophylaxis from before arrival through 7 days after (atovaquone-proguanil). DEET or picaridin.
- Re-check the State Dept Tanzania advisory before paying deposits.
