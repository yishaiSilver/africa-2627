# Kenya minus Kakamega: Istanbul, Ol Pejeta, Lake Baringo, Nakuru, Masai Mara and Naivasha

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=kenya-minus-kakamega

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $5,935 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** the no-crew loop with Kakamega dropped (and with it the Kericho and Narok stopover nights). Every day has a plan; only Dec 31, after the 03:05 landing, is a recovery day.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 15 nights on the ground: 3 Istanbul, 12 Kenya (plus 2 on planes).
- **Route:** Istanbul 3n → Nairobi 1n → Ol Pejeta 2n → **Lake Baringo 2n** → Nakuru 1n → **Masai Mara 2n** (Naboisho conservancy) → Naivasha 3n (with a Soysambu day) → Mount Suswa on the way to an airport hotel 1n.
- **Masai Mara, conservancy only:** walks, night drives and off-road driving with a vehicle cap at Naboisho. The National Reserve day is gone: in the holiday weeks it's the most crowded game viewing on the trip, with vehicles queuing at cat sightings and no off-road. Its day goes to **Soysambu**, a private conservancy on Lake Elmenteita with giraffe, buffalo and flamingos and almost no other visitors.
- **After dark and on foot:** night drives at Ol Pejeta and in the Mara; walks at Ol Pejeta, the Mara conservancy, Crescent Island, Hell's Gate gorge and the Mount Suswa caves.
- **No empty travel days:** Jan 5 reaches Nakuru by 10:00 for a full park day; the last day is Mount Suswa on the way to the airport.
- **Budget: ~$5,935 pp**, ~$192 less than with the reserve day. Biggest items after the Mara camp: the private car to Baringo ($110), the Suswa 4x4 day ($120) and the Ol Pejeta vehicle day ($105).

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

TK10 LAX 19:45 → IST 19:00 next day (~13h). Start shifting to Istanbul time on board.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Istanbul, metro into the city (Istanbul)

Land 19:00. Metro M11 → M2 → T1 to Sultanahmet, ~1.5h door to door, ~₺80–150. Buy an Istanbulkart at the station. Easy dinner, early night.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Sultanahmet on foot (Istanbul)

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern — all within ten minutes of each other. Zero travel.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus (Istanbul)

Topkapı in the morning, Grand Bazaar after lunch, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for dinner. The ferry is the best view in the city and costs almost nothing.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Free morning, fly to Nairobi (Istanbul → in flight)

Spice Bazaar, Süleymaniye or the Archaeological Museum. Bags at the hotel. Leave for the airport by 17:00. TK607 20:30 → NBO 03:05.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B; in-flight

### Thu Dec 31 — Land 03:05, full rest day (Nairobi)

Pre-booked taxi to the guesthouse, ~45m. Room held from Dec 30 so it's ready at 04:00. Sleep to ~11:00, then stay in daylight and no naps after 15:00. Buy a Safaricom SIM. Quiet New Year's Eve.

*Overnight:* Nairobi guesthouse · *Meals:* Own

### Fri Jan 01 — North to Ol Pejeta, first night drive (Nairobi → Ol Pejeta)

Matatu Nairobi → Nanyuki via Thika and Karatina, ~3.5–4.5h with filling time, KSh 700–1,000. Prefer a registered shuttle company over a street matatu. Transfer to the conservancy ~45m. Afternoon at The Stables. 19:00 shared night game drive (~2h, spotlight): lions and hyena on the move, plus a chance of aardwolf, bat-eared fox, porcupine and genet. The hired vehicle is booked for tomorrow. Public holiday: start early, fewer vehicles run.

*Overnight:* The Stables, Ol Pejeta (full board) · *Meals:* L, D Stables

### Sat Jan 02 — Bush walk at 06:30, then a full day of rhino (Ol Pejeta Conservancy)

Zero travel. Guided bush walk 06:30 ($70, max 6). Then a full 8h day in the hired OPC vehicle (The Stables has no vehicles): black and white rhino, Sweetwaters chimps, lion, Grevy's zebra. The last two northern white rhinos (Najin and Fatu) are a separately booked visit, not a drive-by.

*Overnight:* The Stables, Ol Pejeta (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D Stables

### Sun Jan 03 — Thomson's Falls and down into the Rift (Ol Pejeta → Lake Baringo)

Private car from Ol Pejeta via Nyahururu (30-min stop at Thomson's Falls), then down the escarpment to Lake Baringo, ~4.5h with the stop. Arrive for a late lunch. Late afternoon: guided walk along the cliffs behind Kampi ya Samaki with a local bird guide — Baringo has one of Kenya's longest bird lists.

*Overnight:* Lakeside banda, Kampi ya Samaki · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Mon Jan 04 — Dawn boat on Baringo, geysers at Bogoria (Lake Baringo / Lake Bogoria)

06:30 boat on the lake (~2h): fish eagles diving for thrown fish, Nile crocodiles, hippo pods, Goliath herons. Late morning taxi to Lake Bogoria (~1h): geysers and boiling hot springs on the shore, flamingos when the water suits them, and a chance of greater kudu along the western shore. Back to Baringo by dusk.

*Overnight:* Lakeside banda, Kampi ya Samaki · *Meals:* Own

### Tue Jan 05 — Early to Nakuru, full afternoon in the park (Lake Baringo → Lake Nakuru)

06:30 matatu Kampi ya Samaki → Marigat → Nakuru (~2.5h). Hired vehicle with driver from the hotel at ~10:00 (book by phone the day before), in the park until the 18:30 close: white rhino near-certain, black rhino a good chance, Rothschild's giraffe, flamingos if the lake allows. Entry $90 on eCitizen (cashless).

*Overnight:* Nakuru town hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Wed Jan 06 — Over the Mau to the Masai Mara (Nakuru → Narok → Masai Mara)

Early matatu Nakuru → Narok (~2.5h). The camp collects you at Narok around midday (~2.5h). Lunch in camp, afternoon drive; ask for the night drive tonight so tomorrow is free for the full day.

*Overnight:* Basecamp Wilderness, Naboisho (Masai Mara) · *Meals:* L, D camp

### Thu Jan 07 — Walking safari, full day out, night drive (Masai Mara (Naboisho conservancy))

Zero travel. Guided walking safari at dawn (allowed in conservancies, banned in the National Reserve), then out all day with a packed lunch: big cats, topi, plains game, off-road with a vehicle cap. Night drive for serval, genet, porcupine if not done last night. Confirm walk and night drive are included in writing.

*Overnight:* Basecamp Wilderness, Naboisho (Masai Mara) · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 08 — Last dawn in Naboisho, up the Rift to Naivasha (Masai Mara → Narok → Naivasha)

Dawn walk or drive in Naboisho (whichever you didn't get yesterday), breakfast, then the camp vehicle to Narok (~2.5h) and a matatu via Mai Mahiu to Naivasha (~2.5–3h, maybe a change at Mai Mahiu). ~5.5h door to door. Lakeshore camp by late afternoon; hippos on the lawn after dark.

*Overnight:* Camp Carnelley's or Fisherman's Camp, Naivasha · *Meals:* B camp; L, D own

### Sat Jan 09 — Soysambu: giraffe, buffalo and flamingos, almost no one else (Soysambu Conservancy (Lake Elmenteita))

Hired car with driver from Naivasha at 07:00, ~45 min north to Lake Elmenteita. Soysambu is a private ranch conservancy on the lake, with very few vehicles: Rothschild's giraffe, zebra, buffalo, eland, gazelles, and often flamingos and pelicans on the soda lake; leopard and hyena are present but shy. Ask at the gate where you can walk with a ranger. Back at Naivasha by early afternoon; slow evening by the lake. Entry ~$47 for non-residents (2025 operator listing); confirm.

*Overnight:* Camp Carnelley's or Fisherman's Camp, Naivasha · *Meals:* Own

### Sun Jan 10 — Hell's Gate at dawn, Crescent Island in the afternoon (Hell's Gate NP / Crescent Island)

At Elsa Gate for the 06:00 opening, when zebra, buffalo, eland and giraffe are out on the road. Cycle ~7 km past Fischer's Tower to Ol Njorowa gorge, then the Lower Gorge with a licensed guide: a scramble down a narrow red canyon past hot springs and steam vents. Out by ~12:00. Afternoon boat on the lake (hippos, fish eagles) to Crescent Island: walk freely among zebra, giraffe, waterbuck and wildebeest.

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* Own

### Mon Jan 11 — Mount Suswa, then on to the airport (Mount Suswa → Nairobi (JKIA))

Hired 4x4 with driver from Naivasha at 06:30 with your bags, via Mai Mahiu and a rough track up the volcano (~2–2.5h). Hike to the outer crater rim with a community guide (~2.5h round trip), then a guided walk into the lava-tube caves (~1.5h; bats, collapsed skylights; bring a headtorch). Then ~2–2.5h to a hotel near JKIA by ~17:30. Shower, repack, early night.

*Overnight:* Hotel near JKIA · *Meals:* Packed lunch; D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Fly home (Nairobi → Los Angeles)

Hotel shuttle 03:00. TK608 NBO 04:50 → IST, then TK9 → LAX, arriving ~18:15 the same day. About 25h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Two separate 4h drives at Ol Pejeta** (+$110 pp): An afternoon drive on arrival plus a morning drive, instead of one 8h day. Same total time, two vehicle hires.
- **Already have yellow fever shot** (−$200 pp): Kenya does not require yellow fever for arrivals from the US. Check your records before paying for it.
- **Shared game-drive vehicle at Ol Pejeta** (−$110 pp): Doubtful: OPC's tariff lists shared vehicles only for night game drives and lion tracking. Ask The Stables, but don't count on it.
- **Comprehensive insurance with trip cancellation** (+$150 pp): Back to the fact-check's policy. Worth it mainly for the prepaid Naboisho camp; ordinary cancellation cover does not pay out for fear of Ebola.
- **Add the Topkapı Harem** (+$25 pp): 
- **Third night at Ol Pejeta (trip one day longer)** (+$250 pp): Another 24h entry and Stables night, with dawn lion tracking ($70) and time for the northern white rhino visit.
- **Matatus to Baringo instead of the private car** (−$95 pp): Three or four changes via Nyahururu and Nakuru, 6h+ — over the 5h cap.
- **Put the Masai Mara National Reserve day back** (+$190 pp): A full day in the reserve with a private guide (Mara River, open plains) and a night at a Talek camp, instead of Soysambu and the third Naivasha night. Busiest game viewing of the trip in the holiday weeks.
- **Crater Lake Sanctuary instead of Soysambu** (−$60 pp): Walk unguided among giraffe and zebra around a small green crater lake with colobus monkeys, on Naivasha's south shore. ~$33 entry and a short taxi; smaller and tamer than Soysambu.

## Practical

## Entry
- Kenya eTA $30, **etakenya.go.ke only** (not etas.gov.ke); lookalike sites overcharge. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- Türkiye is visa-free for US tourists.

## Packing
- **No camping gear needed.** Every night is a room, a banda (cabin) or the Mara's permanent tented camp, which has real beds and an en-suite bathroom.
- **One bag each you can carry and lift onto a roof rack.** About seven matatu legs; no wheeled cases.
- Ventilated low hikers plus **short gaiters** for grass seeds on the Ol Pejeta and Mara walks.
- Headtorch and a power bank.
- A fleece. Laikipia and Nakuru nights run 8–12°C and dawn drives are genuinely cold. Istanbul is 4–9°C and wet.
- **No camouflage or military-style clothing**: wearing anything resembling police/military uniform is illegal and police enforce it. Plastic bags are banned, including ones brought in (duty-free bags get confiscated).

## Money
- ~130 KES to the dollar. **Carry both cash and cards.** All KWS payments are cashless through eCitizen; matatus, bodas, bike hire and tips are cash only.
- Safaricom SIM at the airport with your passport, then set up M-Pesa.
- Crisp post-2013 USD bills for tips.

## Fees
- The October 2025 KWS increase is still in court, but **the new rates are being charged**: Nakuru $90, Hell's Gate $50 (+~$10 cycling). Lake Bogoria is a county reserve ($50), not KWS.
- Ol Pejeta and the Mara conservancies are not KWS and run separate, cashless systems.
- Ol Pejeta entry covers 24h for overnight guests, but day visitors pay again on re-entry.

## Road reality
- **Nothing moves after dark.** Rural matatus stop in the late afternoon. Every leg is built for a morning start.
- Longest days are Jan 8 (~5.5h) and the Suswa day (Jan 11). Baringo is hot (30–35°C at midday): boats at dawn, walks early or late.
- Jan 1 is a public holiday — matatus run fuller and less often.

## Health and safety
- Kenya is **Level 2** (reissued Jul 2026). No Do-Not-Travel areas on this route.
- **Matatus:** the State Dept tells travelers to avoid matatus and boda-bodas, and US government staff may not use them. The main risk is **crashes**, not just theft. Prefer registered coach/shuttle companies, daylight only, and get your insurer to confirm in writing that matatu and boda travel is covered.
- **Yellow fever:** not required for US arrivals, but **CDC-recommended** for this route (everything outside Nairobi city and the coast).
- **Ebola:** Kenya confirmed an imported Bundibugyo case on Oct 6 2026 (patient from DRC via Kampala, died in Nairobi). Nakuru was named a high-risk preparedness county. Re-check the advisory before paying deposits; prefer refundable bookings.
- **Mpox:** active in Nakuru and Nairobi.
- Malaria prophylaxis from before arrival through 7 days after. DEET.
- AMREF Maisha Bronze ($10) covers 15 days: start it Dec 31; it covers the whole Kenya stay.
