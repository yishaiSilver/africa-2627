# Kenya minus Kakamega: Istanbul, Ol Pejeta, Nakuru, Masai Mara (Naboisho) and a long Naivasha stay

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=kenya-minus-kakamega

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-15 · **Planned cost:** $5,764 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** the no-crew loop with Kakamega dropped, so the Kericho and Narok stopover nights go too. Ol Pejeta, Nakuru, Naboisho and Naivasha all stay.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Fri Jan 15. 18 nights on the ground: 3 Istanbul, 15 Kenya (plus 2 on planes).
- **Route:** Istanbul 3n → Nairobi 1n → Ol Pejeta 2n → **Nakuru 3n** → **Masai Mara** (Naboisho conservancy) 2n → **Naivasha 5n** → Nairobi (Karen) 2n.
- **Less moving:** in Kenya, 6 bases instead of 9 and 6 travel days instead of 8; about 22 road hours instead of 32. Every stay is now 2+ nights.
- **Where the freed nights went:** Nakuru +1 (a rest day), Naivasha +2 (a Mount Longonot volcano climb and a free day), and Karen for the last two nights instead of an airport hotel, with a dawn Nairobi National Park drive and the Sheldrick elephant orphanage on the last day. Home a day earlier (Jan 15).
- **Budget: ~$5,764 pp** vs $5,579 for the no-crew loop. Kakamega was the cheapest part of the trip per night, so the swap doesn't save much.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

TK10 LAX 19:45 → IST 19:00 next day (~13h). Start shifting to Istanbul time on board.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Istanbul, metro into the city (Istanbul)

Land 19:00. Metro M11 → M2 → T1 to Sultanahmet, ~1.5h door to door, ~₺80–150. Buy an Istanbulkart at the station. Easy dinner, early night.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Sultanahmet on foot (Istanbul)

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern — all within ten minutes of each other. Zero travel.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus (Istanbul)

Topkapı in the morning, Grand Bazaar after lunch, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for dinner. The ferry is the best view in the city and costs almost nothing.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Free morning, fly to Nairobi (Istanbul → in flight)

Spice Bazaar, Süleymaniye or the Archaeological Museum. Bags at the hotel. Leave for the airport by 17:00. TK607 20:30 → NBO 03:05.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B; in-flight

### Thu Dec 31 — Land 03:05, full rest day (Nairobi)

Pre-booked taxi to the guesthouse, ~45m. Room held from Dec 30 so it's ready at 04:00. Sleep to ~11:00, then stay in daylight and no naps after 15:00. Buy a Safaricom SIM. Quiet New Year's Eve.

*Overnight:* Nairobi guesthouse · *Meals:* Own

### Fri Jan 01 — Matatu north to Laikipia (Nairobi → Ol Pejeta)

Matatu Nairobi → Nanyuki via Thika and Karatina, ~3.5–4.5h with filling time, KSh 700–1,000. Prefer a registered shuttle company over a street matatu. Transfer to the conservancy ~45m. Afternoon at The Stables; the hired vehicle is booked for tomorrow. Public holiday: start early, fewer vehicles run.

*Overnight:* The Stables, Ol Pejeta (full board) · *Meals:* L, D Stables

### Sat Jan 02 — Bush walk at 06:30, then a full day of rhino (Ol Pejeta Conservancy)

Zero travel. Guided bush walk 06:30 ($70, max 6). Then a full 8h day in the hired OPC vehicle (The Stables has no vehicles): black and white rhino, Sweetwaters chimps, lion, Grevy's zebra. The last two northern white rhinos (Najin and Fatu) are a separately booked visit, not a drive-by.

*Overnight:* The Stables, Ol Pejeta (full board) · *Meals:* B, L, D Stables

### Sun Jan 03 — Matatu to Nakuru via Thomson's Falls (Ol Pejeta → Lake Nakuru)

Transfer to Nanyuki, matatu Nanyuki → Nyahururu (~1.5–2h), a short stop at Thomson's Falls, then Nyahururu → Nakuru (~1.5h). Three connections with bags, the trip's most awkward day. A private car (~$75 pp) replaces all three if the day looks grim: see Alternatives. Arrange tomorrow's park vehicle on arrival.

*Overnight:* Nakuru town hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Mon Jan 04 — Full day in the park (Lake Nakuru NP)

Zero travel. Dawn start in a hired vehicle with driver (no walking access except Baboon Cliff and picnic sites). White rhino near-certain, black rhino a good chance, Rothschild's giraffe, flamingos if the lake allows. Tree-climbing lions are occasional, not reliable. Entry $90 on eCitizen (cashless).

*Overnight:* Nakuru town hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Tue Jan 05 — Rest day (Nakuru)

Zero transfer. Laundry, sleep, a long lunch after five straight days of early starts. Option: a second Lake Nakuru park day instead.

*Overnight:* Nakuru town hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Wed Jan 06 — Over the Mau to the Masai Mara (Nakuru → Narok → Masai Mara)

Early matatu Nakuru → Narok (~2.5h). The camp collects you at Narok around midday (~2.5h). Lunch in camp, afternoon drive; ask for the night drive tonight so tomorrow is free for the full day.

*Overnight:* Basecamp Wilderness, Naboisho (Masai Mara) · *Meals:* L, D camp

### Thu Jan 07 — Walking safari, full day out, night drive (Masai Mara (Naboisho conservancy))

Zero travel. Guided walking safari at dawn (allowed in conservancies, banned in the National Reserve), then out all day with a packed lunch: big cats, topi, plains game, off-road with a vehicle cap. Night drive for serval, genet, porcupine if not done last night. Confirm walk and night drive are included in writing.

*Overnight:* Basecamp Wilderness, Naboisho (Masai Mara) · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 08 — Rift Valley lakes (Masai Mara → Naivasha)

Last dawn drive, breakfast, camp transfer to Narok (~2h), then matatu via Mai Mahiu to Naivasha (~2.5–3h, maybe a change at Mai Mahiu). ~5h door to door: at the cap. Lakeshore camp by late afternoon.

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* B camp; L, D own

### Sat Jan 09 — Hell's Gate by bike (Naivasha / Hell's Gate)

Zero transfer. Hire a bike at Elsa Gate and ride the gorge road. Entry $50 + cycling fee ~$10. A licensed guide is mandatory for the Lower Gorge walk (since 2024). Slow afternoon by the lake.

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* Own

### Sun Jan 10 — Crescent Island and a rest day (Lake Naivasha)

Zero transfer. Morning boat on the lake and the Crescent Island walk, where you walk freely among zebra, giraffe and waterbuck. Afternoon off.

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* Own

### Mon Jan 11 — Climb Mount Longonot (Mount Longonot)

Early matatu to Longonot town (~45m), boda to the gate. Steep 1–1.5h climb to the rim of a 2,776 m volcano, then the full crater-rim circuit (~13.5 km, 4–6h total): sheer drops into a forested caldera with steam vents, Rift Valley views to Lake Naivasha, and zebra, giraffe and buffalo on the lower slopes. Start by 08:00; it gets hot and there's no water on the mountain. KWS recommends a ranger ($15–25 per group).

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* Own

### Tue Jan 12 — Free day (Lake Naivasha)

Zero transfer. Rest legs after Longonot; hippos on the lawn. Option: a 2h horseback ride among giraffe, zebra and wildebeest at Sanctuary Farm.

*Overnight:* Naivasha lakeshore banda · *Meals:* Own

### Wed Jan 13 — Back to Nairobi (Naivasha → Nairobi (Karen))

Shuttle or private car to Karen, ~2.5–3h. Not a matatu for the leg before a flight day. Afternoon free; early night for the dawn drive.

*Overnight:* Karen guesthouse · *Meals:* Own

### Thu Jan 14 — Nairobi National Park at dawn, then the elephant orphanage (Nairobi (Karen))

Zero transfer. Hired vehicle with driver at the Langata gate for 06:00: black and white rhino, lion, cheetah, buffalo and giraffe with the city skyline behind them. The Sheldrick elephant orphanage is inside the park: the 11:00–12:00 public visit (book by email; $20 cash on the day) is covered by the same park ticket. Afternoon repack. Early night; taxi booked for 02:45.

*Overnight:* Karen guesthouse · *Meals:* Own

### Fri Jan 15 — Fly home (Nairobi → Los Angeles)

Taxi 02:45 to JKIA (~40m at that hour). TK608 NBO 04:50 → IST, then TK9 → LAX, arriving ~18:15 the same day. About 25h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Private car Ol Pejeta → Nakuru via Thomson's Falls** (+$67 pp): Replaces three matatu connections on the most awkward day (~3.5h with the falls stop). The US State Department advises against matatus because of crash risk.
- **Two separate 4h drives at Ol Pejeta** (+$110 pp): An afternoon drive on arrival plus a morning drive, instead of one 8h day. Same total time, two vehicle hires.
- **Already have yellow fever shot** (−$200 pp): Kenya does not require yellow fever for arrivals from the US. Check your records before paying for it.
- **Shared game-drive vehicle at Ol Pejeta** (−$110 pp): Doubtful: OPC's tariff lists shared vehicles only for night game drives and lion tracking. Ask The Stables, but don't count on it.
- **Second park day at Nakuru instead of the rest day** (+$145 pp): A second 24h ticket plus another day of hired vehicle, on Jan 5.
- **Third Naboisho night instead of a third Naivasha night** (+$580 pp): Another full conservancy day at ~$630 pppn, less a Naivasha banda night and a day of food.
- **Comprehensive insurance with trip cancellation** (+$150 pp): Back to the fact-check's policy. Worth it mainly for the prepaid Naboisho camp; ordinary cancellation cover does not pay out for fear of Ebola.

## Practical

## Entry
- Kenya eTA $30, **etakenya.go.ke only** (not etas.gov.ke); lookalike sites overcharge. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- Türkiye is visa-free for US tourists.

## Packing
- **One bag each you can carry and lift onto a roof rack.** About seven matatu legs; no wheeled cases.
- Ventilated low hikers plus **short gaiters** for grass seeds on the Ol Pejeta and Mara walks.
- Headtorch and a power bank.
- A fleece. Laikipia and Nakuru nights run 8–12°C and dawn drives are genuinely cold. Istanbul is 4–9°C and wet.
- **No camouflage or military-style clothing**: wearing anything resembling police/military uniform is illegal and police enforce it. Plastic bags are banned, including ones brought in (duty-free bags get confiscated).

## Money
- ~130 KES to the dollar. **Carry both cash and cards.** All KWS payments are cashless through eCitizen; matatus, bodas, bike hire and tips are cash only.
- Safaricom SIM at the airport with your passport, then set up M-Pesa.
- Crisp post-2013 USD bills for tips.

## Fees
- The October 2025 KWS increase is still in court, but **the new rates are being charged**: Nakuru $90, Hell's Gate and Longonot $50 (+~$10 cycling at Hell's Gate).
- Ol Pejeta and the Mara conservancies are not KWS and run separate, cashless systems.
- Ol Pejeta entry covers 24h for overnight guests, but day visitors pay again on re-entry.

## Road reality
- **Nothing moves after dark.** Rural matatus stop in the late afternoon. Every leg is built for a morning start.
- Longest days are ~5h (Jan 6, Jan 8). Ten days in Kenya involve no travel.
- Jan 1 is a public holiday — matatus run fuller and less often.

## Health and safety
- Kenya is **Level 2** (reissued Jul 2026). No Do-Not-Travel areas on this route.
- **Matatus:** the State Dept tells travelers to avoid matatus and boda-bodas, and US government staff may not use them. The main risk is **crashes**, not just theft. Prefer registered coach/shuttle companies, daylight only, and get your insurer to confirm in writing that matatu and boda travel is covered.
- **Yellow fever:** not required for US arrivals, but **CDC-recommended** for this route (everything outside Nairobi city and the coast).
- **Ebola:** Kenya confirmed an imported Bundibugyo case on Oct 6 2026 (patient from DRC via Kampala, died in Nairobi). Nakuru was named a high-risk preparedness county. Re-check the advisory before paying deposits; prefer refundable bookings.
- **Mpox:** active in Nakuru and Nairobi.
- Malaria prophylaxis from before arrival through 7 days after. DEET.
- AMREF Maisha Bronze ($10) covers 15 days: start it Dec 31 so it runs through Jan 14.

## Open questions

1. ~$5,764 pp: OK, or trade a Naivasha night for something?
2. Insurance: medical + evacuation only, or pay ~$150 more for cancellation cover on the prepaid camp?
3. Five Naivasha nights: restful, or too many? The third Naboisho night is the obvious swap if budget allows.
4. Are you already vaccinated for yellow fever? It is CDC-recommended for this route.
