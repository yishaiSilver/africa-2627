# Madagascar: Amber Mountain, Ankarana, Nosy Be diving and Andasibe

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=madagascar

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $4,960 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** northern and eastern Madagascar for **mammals and herps**, with three days of diving on Nosy Be. Every forest stop has night walks, which is where most chameleons, leaf-tailed geckos and mouse lemurs are found.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 15 nights on the ground, 2 on planes.
- **Route:** Tana 1n → ✈ Diego Suarez → **Montagne d'Ambre 2n** → **Ankarana 2n** → boat to **Nosy Be 5n** (6 dives + Lokobe) → ✈ Tana 1n → **Andasibe 4n** (Analamazaotra, Mitsinjo, Mantadia, Vohimana) → home.
- **Season:** the start of the rains. That is the **best time for herps** (frogs breeding, chameleons active) and for lemurs with young; the cost is afternoon rain, muddy tracks, and some cyclone risk from January. Diving is done in late December/early January, before most Nosy Be centres slow down.
- **Mammals:** about 15 lemur species likely (indri, diademed sifaka, crowned, Sanford's, black, sportive and mouse lemurs), plus tenrecs. **No fossa** on this route; the Kirindy option adds the best odds for one.
- **Getting around:** a private driver for both road sections, two domestic flights, and a buffer night after each flight because domestic schedules shift.
- **Budget: ~$4,960 pp**, including ~$150 contingency. International flights are the swing item: $1,600–2,400 at Christmas.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Madagascar (Los Angeles → in flight)

Air France via Paris or Turkish via Istanbul, ~30–34h with the connection. Price both; Turkish flies to Antananarivo only on some days of the week. Avoid routings with a 12h+ layover in Addis Ababa or Nairobi, which would trigger a yellow fever certificate check.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land in Antananarivo (in flight → Antananarivo)

Land in the evening. Visa check at Ivato (e-visa printed), change cash at the airport bureau, buy a Telma or Orange SIM. Hotel shuttle to a guesthouse near the airport, not into the city. Antananarivo (Tana) is UTC+3.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse near Ivato airport · *Meals:* In-flight; D hotel

### Mon Dec 28 — North to Amber Mountain, first night walk (Antananarivo → Diego Suarez → Montagne d'Ambre)

Morning domestic flight Tana → Diego Suarez (~2h). The driver meets you at the airport; ~1h up to Joffreville at ~1,000 m, where it's cooler and wet. After dark, a guided night walk along the park boundary road: leaf-tailed geckos (Uroplatus finiavana) on trunks, sleeping chameleons on twig tips, frogs calling.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Joffreville · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D lodge

### Tue Dec 29 — Amber Mountain: Brookesia, chameleons, lemurs (Montagne d'Ambre NP)

Zero travel. Full day in the montane rainforest with a park guide: the Amber Mountain chameleon (Calumma ambreense), Brookesia leaf chameleons in the leaf litter, crowned and Sanford's brown lemurs, the Cascade Sacrée and Grande Cascade waterfalls. Back in after dinner for a second night walk inside the park (mouse lemurs, northern rufous mouse lemur, more geckos and frogs). Rain is likely in the afternoon: rain jacket and leech-proof socks.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Joffreville · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D lodge

### Wed Dec 30 — South to the tsingy (Montagne d'Ambre → Ankarana)

~2h on the paved RN6 to Mahamasina at Ankarana's east gate. Late-afternoon walk on the short circuits for crowned lemurs and Ankarana sportive lemurs peering out of tree holes, then a night walk: Uroplatus henkeli, Furcifer chameleons, mouse lemurs, snakes on the move after rain.

*Overnight:* Bungalow at Mahamasina, Ankarana east gate · *Meals:* B lodge; L, D own

### Thu Dec 31 — Ankarana: tsingy, caves and crowned lemurs (Ankarana Special Reserve)

Zero travel. Long guided day: the Petit Tsingy limestone pinnacles and hanging bridge, the bat cave, the Perte des Rivières sinkhole, dry forest for lemurs, chameleons and Madagascar ground boas. The western side is closed in the rains; the east-gate circuits stay open. Quiet New Year's Eve at the bungalows.

*Overnight:* Bungalow at Mahamasina, Ankarana east gate · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D lodge

### Fri Jan 01 — Down to the coast and across to Nosy Be (Ankarana → Ankify → Nosy Be)

Early morning walk if the night was quiet, then ~3.5–4h with the driver to Ankify via Ambanja. Speedboat to Nosy Be (~45m). Transfer to a small hotel at Ambondrona or Ambatoloaka, near the dive centre. Public holiday: confirm the boat and the hotel pickup in advance.

*Overnight:* Beach hotel, Ambondrona (Nosy Be) · *Meals:* B lodge; L, D own

### Sat Jan 02 — Dive day 1 (Nosy Be)

Two boat dives with Scuba Nosy Be: reefs and pinnacles off the west coast, with turtles, reef sharks, frogfish and nudibranchs. Bring your Advanced Open Water card and logbook. Afternoon off.

*Overnight:* Beach hotel, Ambondrona (Nosy Be) · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Sun Jan 03 — Lokobe: black lemurs and tiny chameleons (Lokobe (Nosy Be))

Zero diving. Half-day guided trip by road to Ampasipohy and pirogue into Lokobe, Nosy Be's last primary forest: black lemurs (males black, females russet), the Nosy Be sportive lemur, panther chameleons, Brookesia stumpffi on the forest floor, Uroplatus ebenaui and boas. Back for an evening walk around the hotel for panther chameleons in the bushes.

*Overnight:* Beach hotel, Ambondrona (Nosy Be) · *Meals:* B hotel; L tour; D own

### Mon Jan 04 — Dive day 2: Nosy Tanikely (Nosy Be)

Two dives around the Nosy Tanikely marine reserve: turtles on almost every dive, big schools, healthy hard coral. Late December is the end of the whale shark season; ask the dive centre to watch for them on the boat ride.

*Overnight:* Beach hotel, Ambondrona (Nosy Be) · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Tue Jan 05 — Dive day 3 (Nosy Be)

Two last dives, chosen with the dive centre for conditions (offshore banks if calm). Afternoon off; dry the gear and repack. 24h no-fly gap before tomorrow's flight: finish the second dive by ~12:00.

*Overnight:* Beach hotel, Ambondrona (Nosy Be) · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Wed Jan 06 — Fly back to Tana (Nosy Be → Antananarivo)

Afternoon flight Nosy Be → Tana (~1.5h), more than 24h after the last dive. Night near Ivato. This night is also the buffer: domestic flights in Madagascar are often rescheduled, and you still have five days before the flight home.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse near Ivato airport · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Thu Jan 07 — East to the rainforest (Antananarivo → Andasibe)

Driver at 07:00; ~3.5–4h east on the RN2 to Andasibe. Afternoon in the Mitsinjo community reserve (indri groups habituated by local guides). First Andasibe night walk along the road edge: mouse lemurs, Parson's and short-horned chameleons, Boophis tree frogs, and leaf-tailed geckos.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Andasibe · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D lodge

### Fri Jan 08 — Indri morning (Analamazaotra (Andasibe))

Zero travel. 07:00 in Analamazaotra for the indri song at its loudest, plus common brown lemurs, eastern lesser bamboo lemurs, giraffe weevils. Afternoon rest; rain is likely. Night walk in Mitsinjo or Vohimana with a herp-focused guide: ask for Uroplatus phantasticus and sikorae, Brookesia, and mantella frogs.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Andasibe · *Meals:* B, D lodge; L own

### Sat Jan 09 — Mantadia: diademed sifaka and deep forest (Mantadia NP)

Zero transfer. Full day in Mantadia (~45 min up a rough track): diademed sifaka, black-and-white ruffed lemur, rainforest birds, streams with mantella frogs and Madagascar tree boas. Packed lunch. The track can be slippery in January: keep the 4x4.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Andasibe · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D lodge

### Sun Jan 10 — Vohimana: herps all day (Vohimana (Andasibe))

Zero transfer. Morning and late afternoon in the Vohimana reserve (~15 min east), one of the best herp sites in the east: mantella frogs, Calumma chameleons, lowland streaked tenrecs, leaf-tailed geckos. Last night walk.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Andasibe · *Meals:* B, D lodge; L own

### Mon Jan 11 — Back to Tana, fly home (Andasibe → Antananarivo → in flight)

Morning walk if wanted, then ~3.5–4h back to Ivato with a margin for Tana traffic. Day room near the airport to shower and repack. Late-evening flight to Paris or Istanbul.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B lodge; L, D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Arrive home (in flight → Los Angeles)

Connect in Paris or Istanbul; arrive LAX the same day (~30h door to door).

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Kirindy for fossa (swap 2 Nosy Be days)** (+$350 pp): Fly Tana → Morondava and spend 2 nights at Kirindy: fossa (best odds in Madagascar), giant jumping rat, Verreaux's sifaka, the Avenue of the Baobabs. Costs two dives and the Tanikely day. The dirt road can close after heavy rain.
- **4 more dives (10-dive package)** (+$150 pp): €300 for 10 instead of €200 for 6, plus rental. Fits on the Lokobe day and the Jan 1 afternoon.
- **Whale shark snorkel trip** (+$65 pp): Blue Wave Diving runs these Oct–Dec at ~€55; late December is the tail of the season, so ask first.
- **Ankarana Lodge instead of the gate bungalows** (+$190 pp): Pool and air-conditioning; a drive from the gate for night walks.
- **Specialist herp guide in Andasibe, 2 nights** (+$60 pp): A guide who knows where the Uroplatus phantasticus and Brookesia roost; ~$30 per night per group on top of the normal guide.
- **Comprehensive insurance with trip cancellation** (+$110 pp): Instead of medical + evacuation only.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Madagascar e-visa:** €35 for 16–30 days at evisamada-mg.com; print the approval. Passport 6+ months with 2 blank pages.
- **Yellow fever:** not required flying from the US via Paris or Istanbul. A 12h+ transit in Ethiopia or Kenya would require it.

## Packing
- **Rain gear**: a real rain jacket, quick-dry clothes, leech socks for Amber Mountain and Andasibe, and dry bags for cameras.
- **Headtorch each, plus a spare.** Night walks are most of the herp list; a red-light setting helps.
- Boots with grip for muddy trails and the sharp tsingy at Ankarana.
- Dive: your certification card and logbook; rental gear is available. A 3mm wetsuit is plenty (water ~28°C).
- One soft bag each; domestic flights have tight limits and speedboats have no dry hold.

## Money
- **Cash in ariary** for park fees, guides and tips (no cards at park gates). Change euros or dollars at the airport; ATMs in Tana, Diego and Hell-Ville.
- Euros are the easiest foreign currency for dive centres and lodges.

## Season
- Late December to March is the wet season: heavy afternoon showers, warm nights, the best herp activity of the year. Cyclones are possible from January; watch forecasts before the Nosy Be legs.
- Leeches in the eastern and montane rainforests; harmless but persistent.

## Health and safety
- US advisory **Level 2** (Exercise Increased Caution) since Dec 2025, after a Level 3 period during the late-2025 unrest. Crime is the main risk, especially after dark in towns; protests happen in Tana.
- Don't travel by road at night between towns; every drive here is in daylight.
- Malaria prophylaxis from before arrival through 7 days after. Bubonic plague occurs seasonally in rural highland areas; the risk to travellers on this route is very low.
- Medical care outside Tana is basic. Evacuation cover is essential.
