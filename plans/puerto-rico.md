## Itinerary

### Sat Nov 07 — Fly to Puerto Rico (Los Angeles → San Juan)

Early 1-stop (via MIA/CLT/DFW/ATL/JFK). LAX ~06:00–08:00 → SJU ~18:00–21:00 (+4h clock). Taxi 20m to Old San Juan. Street food, sleep.

*Overnight:* Old San Juan guesthouse · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Sun Nov 08 — Forts, then east (Old San Juan → Río Grande)

08:30 El Morro + San Cristóbal (one ~$10 fee). Paseo del Morro walk. Lunch. Taxi to SJU, pick up car ~13:00. ~50m PR-26/PR-3 east. Dusk: coquí chorus at guesthouse.

*Overnight:* Río Grande / Luquillo guesthouse · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Mon Nov 09 — Rainforest + bio bay (new moon) (El Yunque → Laguna Grande)

Gate 08:00 (PR-191 free, no reservation; closes at capacity: go early, Monday quieter). El Portal center ($8). Short trails open (check alerts; La Mina closed). Parrot/tody/lizard-cuckoo watch. Nap. ~18:00 Laguna Grande kayak, ~2h. New moon 02:03 AST today: darkest night. Bed by 22:00: boat tomorrow.

*Overnight:* Río Grande / Luquillo guesthouse · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Tue Nov 10 — Culebra snorkel day (catamaran) (Fajardo → Culebra reefs)

07:45 drive ~35m to Puerto del Rey marina, Fajardo. ~08:30–09:00 catamaran, ~45–50m crossing. Captain picks the calm spot: Tamarindo/Carlos Rosario (west side, Luis Peña reserve: turtles, reef) or Culebrita/Flamenco. 2 snorkel stops, beach time, buffet lunch, drinks, gear. Back ~15:00. Siesta. Easy dinner. Bio bay backup tonight only if Nov 9 rained out. Swell cancels? Fall back to Luquillo swim + kioskos.

*Overnight:* Río Grande / Luquillo guesthouse · *Meals:* B own; L on boat; D own

### Wed Nov 11 — Cross to the southwest (Río Grande → Ponce → La Parguera)

~1h50 PR-30/PR-52 (tolls) to Ponce. Lunch + Parque de Bombas, plaza, cathedral (~2h). ~1h on to La Parguera. Sunset on the water.

*Overnight:* La Parguera inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Thu Nov 12 — Dry forest + endemic birds (Guánica · Cabo Rojo · La Parguera)

06:30 Guánica dry forest (30m): nightjar zone, Adelaide's warbler, lizard-cuckoo, tody. Out before heat (~11:00). Optional 2nd snorkel: Gilligan's Island / Cayo Aurora, 10m boat from Restaurante San Jacinto (~$11; mangrove + seagrass, calm, shallow; no shade/food; confirm open). Siesta. Cabo Rojo salt flats only if fresh (skip = easier day). ~19:30 La Parguera bio bay boat (moon sets early; dark).

*Overnight:* La Parguera inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Fri Nov 13 — Karst cave, back to San Juan (La Parguera → Cueva Ventana → Old San Juan)

~1h50 north (PR-2 or scenic PR-10 via karst). Cueva Ventana tour (~1h, $19). Optional Río Camuy only if confirmed open. ~1h PR-22 to SJU, return car. Taxi to Old San Juan. Farewell dinner.

*Overnight:* Old San Juan guesthouse · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Sat Nov 14 — Fly home (San Juan → Los Angeles)

Coffee walk on the walls. Taxi 20m. 1-stop SJU ~10:00–12:00 → LAX ~17:00–20:00 same day (~10h incl. connect).

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* B; in-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Vieques overnight for Mosquito Bay** (+$120 pp): Brightest bay in the world (Guinness 2006). Swap one Luquillo night for Vieques: ferry from Ceiba (book ahead) or puddle-jumper. Tour $67–105. Leave car in Ceiba; públicos on island. Do on Nov 9 or 10 (dark).
- **No car: San Juan base + day tours** (+$150 pp): Stay all nights in San Juan. El Yunque + bio bay combo tour, Cueva Ventana tour (~$125), Uber. Loses Guánica, La Parguera, Ponce; long pickups. Not cheaper.
- **Culebra DIY: Ceiba ferry + golf cart** (−$110 pp): Same day, own pace: Tamarindo turtles at 08:00, Carlos Rosario, Flamenco. Ferry $2.25/leg + $2 fee; cart ~$75/day shared. Online seats sell out ~a month ahead (residents first); 1h check-in; ~11h day. Or 15m flight Ceiba→Culebra ~$100–110 RT.
- **Keep slow Luquillo day instead** (−$170 pp): Drop Culebra. Sleep in, Luquillo swim, kioskos. Restores the rest day; snorkel only at Gilligan's Nov 12.
- **Half-day endemic birding guide** (+$120 pp): Guánica or Maricao at dawn; or Río Abajo parrot attempt (restricted access needs local guide). Best shot at nightjar + elfin-woods warbler.
- **Return via Toro Negro / Ruta Panorámica** (+$0 pp): Cloud forest, Doña Juana falls, highest peaks. Replaces Cueva Ventana Nov 13. Curvy, slow: ~3.5h+ driving. Over the 3h rule.
- **Upgrade lodging** (+$490 pp): Boutique Old San Juan hotel + rainforest treehouse/eco-lodge + Guánica hotel. ~+$70 pp/night.

## Practical

## Entry
- US citizens: domestic flight. No passport. REAL ID or passport for TSA.

## Flights
- No nonstop LAX–SJU found. 1 stop: AA (MIA/CLT/DFW), Delta (ATL), JetBlue (JFK/BOS/MCO), Spirit/Frontier (MCO/FLL).
- Red-eyes exist (LAX ~22:00 → SJU ~noon next day) but cost a night. Plan: early Sat departure, arrive evening.
- PR = AST (UTC−4), no DST. LA = PST after Nov 1: **+4h** going east.

## Car? Yes.
- El Yunque at 08:00, Fajardo bio bay, southwest (Guánica, La Parguera, Cabo Rojo), Cueva Ventana: no transit. Uber thin outside metro.
- Tours-only = San Juan base, long pickups, no southwest. Costs more.
- No car in Old San Juan nights: narrow streets, scarce parking.
- **AutoExpreso**: all tolls electronic. Rental tag mandatory; fee flat/day or per use + tolls. Ask at pickup. Tolls up slightly Jan 2026.
- Distances in km, speed in mph, gas in liters. Driving assertive; potholes; avoid unlit mountain roads at night.

## Money
- USD. Cards widely; cash for kioskos, tips. NHS forts cashless.
- Tip 15–20%. Bio bay guides ~$10.

## Safety
- Generally fine for tourists. Usual car rules: nothing visible in car, esp. beach/trailheads.
- Bio bay: no sunscreen/repellent before kayak (harms organisms); follow guide. Life vest.
- Flash floods: stay out of rivers when raining upstream (El Yunque).

## Snorkel (Culebra Nov 10, Gilligan's Nov 12)
- Tour: lunch, drinks, mask/fins incl. Bring own mask if picky. Towel not incl.
- Tamarindo, Melones, Carlos Rosario = Canal Luis Peña reserve: no fishing, no touching/taking coral, no standing on reef. Keep ~3 m from turtles; no chasing.
- Reef-safe (mineral, zinc) sunscreen, or better: long-sleeve rash guard + hat. Apply 30m before.
- Nov = north swell season. North beaches (Flamenco, Carlos Rosario edge) can break; west/south (Tamarindo, Melones, Culebrita lee) calmer. Captain decides. Seasick-prone: pill before boarding.
- Water shoes: rocky, urchin entries at Tamarindo/Carlos Rosario.
- Water ~28°C: no wetsuit needed.

## Weather
- Highs ~29°C/84°F, lows ~22–24°C. Short showers, often afternoon. El Yunque wet any day.
- Hurricane season ends Nov 30: low but real risk. Watch NHC; free-cancel bookings.
- Rain/clouds dim bio bays. Bays close in storms.

## Packing
- Rain shell, quick-dry clothes, trail shoes with grip (slick), sandals, swimsuit, headlamp (red light for coquí), binoculars, repellent (mosquitoes + no-see-ums at dusk, esp. La Parguera/Fajardo), reef-safe sunscreen, dry bag.

## Food
- Mofongo, lechón, alcapurrias, bacalaítos. Kiosk lunch $10–15; dinner $25–40 pp. Tap water OK in metro; bottled in rural spots after storms.
