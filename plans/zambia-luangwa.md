# Istanbul and Zambia: South Luangwa in the green season

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=zambia-luangwa

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $5,745 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** three nights in Istanbul to break the journey, then ten nights in one of Africa's best parks for big cats. **South Luangwa** has a lot of leopards, and its afternoon drives run on after dark with a spotlight. Lions are common, and the Luangwa River is packed with hippos and crocodiles. Elephants walk through the Mfuwe camps.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 15 nights on the ground: 3 Istanbul, 2 Lusaka, **10 South Luangwa**, plus 2 on planes. Same dates as the Kenya and Tanzania trips.
- **Route:** Istanbul 3n → ✈ Addis → Lusaka 1n → ✈ Mfuwe → **South Luangwa 10n** (Croc Valley Camp at the Mfuwe gate) → ✈ Lusaka 1n → home via Addis.
- **Flights:** Turkish dropped Lusaka in May 2026, so the plan is one Star Alliance ticket: Turkish LAX → Istanbul, Ethiopian Istanbul → Addis → Lusaka, and Ethiopian home.
- **Walking safaris (a priority):** 4 morning walks with a guide and an armed park scout. South Luangwa is where walking safaris began, but in the rains walks are shorter, stay on higher ground, and get cancelled when the grass is too tall or it's pouring. **Not yet confirmed for January:** Flatdogs only walks June–October, and the Bushcamp Company's walking camps are closed in January. Croc Valley lists walks but not the months, so getting that in writing is the first booking step. If it can't, Shawa Luangwa Camp does walk in the green season, at a much higher price (see the option).
- **Drives:** an afternoon drive every day that turns into a night drive (leopard, lion, hyena, genet, civet, porcupine, owls), plus 3 morning drives. 13 drives and 4 walks in all.
- **Herps:** the rains are the best time: flap-necked chameleons in the spotlight, bullfrogs, monitors, crocodiles, pythons and puff adders crossing the roads at night.
- **Budget: ~$5,745 pp** (≈ $11,490 for two), ~$380 less than Kenya minus Kakamega. Istanbul adds ~$375 on the ground, but the Luangwa stay is 3 nights shorter.

## Crowds and wildlife

1–5, for late December and early January. Crowds: 1 = you rarely see another vehicle, 5 = queues of vehicles at sightings (lower is better). Wildlife numbers: how many animals you see. Wildlife diversity: how many different kinds. These are judgments from how each place works in this season, not counts.

| Stop | Nights | Crowds | Numbers | Diversity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Istanbul | 3 | 5/5 | — | — | Sultanahmet in New Year week is packed. Book Hagia Sophia and Topkapı early, go at opening time. |
| South Luangwa (Mfuwe sector) | 10 | 2/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | Green season: most camps inside the park are closed, so a sighting rarely has more than one or two other vehicles. Lots of hippos, crocodiles, elephants, puku and impala; resident leopards and lions. No rhinos, no cheetah. |
| **Whole trip** | | 2/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | The quietest guided safari of the four, with big animal numbers, but one park and one habitat, so the fewest kinds of animal. |

**Pros**

- Best leopard odds of the four, with a night drive every evening.
- Quietest of the guided trips; one camp for 10 nights, almost no moving.
- Huge hippo and crocodile numbers; the rains bring out chameleons, snakes and frogs.
- Walking safaris where they began (if the camp confirms January walks).

**Cons**

- One park and one habitat: the least variety (no rhinos, no cheetah, few antelope species).
- Wet season: afternoon storms, thick green bush, the odd cancelled drive or walk.
- January walks are not yet confirmed at the budget camps.
- Long journey: Istanbul → Addis → Lusaka → Mfuwe.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

TK10 LAX 19:45 → IST 19:00 next day (~13h). Start shifting to Istanbul time on board.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Istanbul, metro into the city (Istanbul)

Land 19:00. Metro M11 → M2 → T1 to Sultanahmet, ~1.5h door to door. Buy an Istanbulkart at the station. Easy dinner, early night.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Sultanahmet on foot (Istanbul)

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern — all within ten minutes of each other. Zero travel.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus (Istanbul)

Topkapı in the morning, Grand Bazaar after lunch, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for dinner. The ferry is the best view in the city and costs almost nothing.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Fly to Lusaka (Istanbul → Addis Ababa → Lusaka)

Ethiopian Istanbul → Addis → Lusaka (~10–12h with the connection). Pick the connection that lands in Lusaka by Dec 30 evening. If the only good one runs overnight, you land Dec 31 morning and take the later Proflight to Mfuwe instead. Visa-free entry for US citizens (confirm before you fly). Guesthouse near the airport. Lusaka is UTC+2, one hour behind Istanbul.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse near Lusaka airport · *Meals:* B hotel; in-flight; D hotel

### Thu Dec 31 — Fly to Mfuwe, first night drive (Lusaka → Mfuwe → South Luangwa)

Morning Proflight to Mfuwe (~1h15). The camp transfer takes ~40 min to Croc Valley on the Luangwa River, just outside the Mfuwe gate. 16:00 drive into the park: puku, impala, Thornicroft's giraffe, then a spotlight on the way back after dark for leopard, genets and hyena. New Year's Eve at the camp bar over the river, hippos grunting below.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D camp

### Fri Jan 01 — First walking safari (South Luangwa NP)

06:00 walk with a guide and an armed park scout, ~3–4h on higher ground near the river: elephant and buffalo at a respectful distance, hippos in the lagoons, fresh lion and leopard tracks, dung beetles, termite mounds, frogs and chameleons up close. If rain or tall grass rules it out, take a morning drive instead. Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Sat Jan 02 — Second walk (South Luangwa NP)

Morning walk, a different route chosen with the guide (lagoon edges, the mopane woodland). Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Sun Jan 03 — Morning drive for lions (South Luangwa NP)

06:00 morning drive, the best time for lions on a kill and for wild dog, which are possible in the green season. Back by 10:00 before the heat. Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Mon Jan 04 — Third walk (South Luangwa NP)

Morning walk. Afternoon and night drive, concentrating on the leopard areas the guides have been tracking.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Tue Jan 05 — Rest morning, night drive (South Luangwa NP)

Rest morning in camp: elephants and hippos at the river, vervets and baboons, laundry. Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Wed Jan 06 — Fourth walk (South Luangwa NP)

Morning walk. Afternoon and night drive. Bring a headtorch on the camp paths after dark (always with a guard): frogs, geckos and the occasional snake after rain.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Thu Jan 07 — Rest morning, night drive (South Luangwa NP)

Rest morning, or a walk to the Tribal Textiles workshop nearby. Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Fri Jan 08 — Morning and night drives (South Luangwa NP)

Morning drive and afternoon/night drive. Ask the guide to work the lagoons and oxbows (Wamilombe, Mfuwe lagoon) for big hippo pods and crocodiles.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Sat Jan 09 — Last night drive (South Luangwa NP)

Rest morning (or a make-up walk if one was rained off). Last afternoon and night drive.

### Sun Jan 10 — Last morning drive, fly to Lusaka (South Luangwa → Mfuwe → Lusaka)

Last morning drive, brunch, then the transfer to Mfuwe airport and the afternoon Proflight to Lusaka. Night near the airport, as a buffer for Proflight schedule changes.

## Options & swaps

- **2 nights at Shawa Luangwa Camp for confirmed green-season walks** (+$1,300 pp): Green Safaris' camp, which runs walks in the green season. Fully inclusive at ~$760 pp/night (Nov 2026 rate), likely more over the holidays; replaces 2 Croc Valley nights. Only needed if Croc Valley can't walk in January.
- **Walk every morning instead of 3 drives and 3 rest mornings** (+$300 pp): Up to 9 walks if the camp, the grass and the rain allow. ~$70 each plus the park fee on rest days.
- **Kafue: 3 nights at Mukambi instead of 3 in Luangwa** (+$750 pp): Mukambi Safari Lodge is the one camp in central Kafue that stays open in the rains: a different, wilder park with leopard and lion, ~4h by road from Lusaka. ~$380 pp/night full board plus the road transfer. No walks there in the wet months.
- **Fly into Lilongwe and drive to Mfuwe** (−$250 pp): Ethiopian also flies to Lilongwe, ~4.5–5h by road from Mfuwe via Chipata. Replaces Proflight and the Lusaka nights with a road transfer (~$300 per vehicle each way). Needs a Malawi visa ($50), and Malawi's fuel shortage makes that leg less certain.
- **Skip Istanbul: 13 Luangwa nights instead** (−$240 pp): The earlier version of this trip: fly straight to Lusaka and spend the three Istanbul nights in South Luangwa.

## Booking timeline

- **Now (Oct 24–31):** Email Croc Valley first: do guided walks run Jan 1–6, what do they cost, and how often are they cancelled in January? If not, ask Green Safaris about Shawa Luangwa for 2 nights. Also: 10 nights from Dec 31, 13 drives, meal prices, airport transfers.
- **Once walks are confirmed:** Book the multi-city ticket (TK LAX → IST Dec 26; Ethiopian IST → LUN Dec 30; Ethiopian LUN → LAX Jan 11) and Proflight Lusaka → Mfuwe Dec 31 and Mfuwe → Lusaka Jan 10.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Türkiye:** visa-free for US citizens (90 days).
- **Zambia:** visa-free for US citizens per 2026 sources (30–90 days; the sources disagree). Passport valid 6+ months with 2 blank pages; carry proof of the onward flight.
- **Yellow fever:** not required arriving via Istanbul; a transit in Addis under 12h doesn't trigger it.

## Packing
- **For the walks:** neutral colours (khaki, olive, brown; no white or bright colours), closed shoes with grip, long trousers for grass and ticks, a hat, 1L of water each.
- Warm layer for night drives (open vehicles, wind after rain); rain jacket.
- Headtorch each; hippos and elephants walk through camp at night, so always walk with a guard after dark.
- Binoculars; a fast lens for spotlight work (no flash at animals).
- Layers for Istanbul: late December is ~5–10 °C and often wet.

## Money
- Turkish lira for Istanbul (cards work almost everywhere).
- US dollars at the camp; **park fees in cash** (no cards at the gate). Kwacha for small purchases; ATMs in Lusaka and Mfuwe.

## Season
- Late December to January is Luangwa's wet season: hot (~30–35 °C), humid, heavy afternoon storms. Walks go out early and stay on higher ground, and may be cut short or switched to a drive. Some interior roads close; the Mfuwe sector loops stay open. Proflight sometimes shifts times in the rains; that's why the plan has a Lusaka night at both ends.

## Health and safety
- Check travel.state.gov for Türkiye and Zambia before booking.
- On walks: single file, stay behind the scout, never run. The guides brief you first.
- Malaria is high-risk in the rains: prophylaxis plus long sleeves and repellent at dusk.
- The nearest good hospital is in Lusaka or Johannesburg; evacuation cover is essential.
