# Zambia: South Luangwa in the green season

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=zambia-luangwa

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $5,505 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** almost the whole trip in one of Africa's best parks for big cats, in the green season. **South Luangwa** has a lot of leopards, and its afternoon drives run on after dark with a spotlight. Lions are common, and the Luangwa River is packed with hippos and crocodiles. Elephants walk through the Mfuwe camps.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 15 nights on the ground: 2 in Lusaka, **13 in South Luangwa**, plus 2 on planes.
- **Route:** Lusaka 1n → ✈ Mfuwe → **South Luangwa 13n** (Croc Valley Camp at the Mfuwe gate) → ✈ Lusaka 1n → home.
- **Green season:** the bush is green, impala and puku have young (so the cats are busy), and the park is nearly empty. Many camps deep inside the park close; the Mfuwe-gate camps stay open, and the main loops near the Mfuwe bridge stay drivable. Expect afternoon storms and the odd cancelled drive. Thirteen nights gives plenty of slack.
- **Drives:** an afternoon drive every day that turns into a night drive (leopard, lion, hyena, genet, civet, porcupine, owls), and morning drives on 5 days. That's 18 drives, about $60 each plus $25 a day in park fees. Rest mornings in camp are for the elephants and hippos at the river and for the heat.
- **Herps:** the rains are the best time: flap-necked chameleons in the spotlight, bullfrogs, monitors, crocodiles, pythons and puff adders crossing the roads at night.
- **No Lake Malawi:** dropped in favour of safari days. Malawi is in a fuel crisis (Oct 2026) and Americans need a visa again. Two lake nights stay as an option.
- **Budget: ~$5,505 pp** (≈ $11,010 for two). The ~$500 over $5k is the price of 13 nights. Cut 3 Luangwa nights (−$600) or fly in via Lilongwe (−$250) to get under it.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Lusaka (Los Angeles → in flight)

Ethiopian via Addis Ababa, or Qatar via Doha or Emirates via Dubai, ~28–32h with connections. Pick a routing that lands in Lusaka by Dec 27 evening.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land in Lusaka (in flight → Lusaka)

Visa-free entry for US citizens (confirm before you fly). Change some dollars to kwacha, buy an Airtel or MTN SIM, and stay at a guesthouse near the airport. Lusaka is UTC+2.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse near Lusaka airport · *Meals:* In-flight; D hotel

### Mon Dec 28 — Fly to Mfuwe, first night drive (Lusaka → Mfuwe → South Luangwa)

Morning Proflight to Mfuwe (~1h15). The camp transfer takes ~40 min to Croc Valley on the Luangwa River, just outside the Mfuwe gate. Hippos grunting in front of camp. 16:00 drive into the park: puku, impala, Thornicroft's giraffe, then a spotlight on the way back after dark for leopard, genets and hyena.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D camp

### Tue Dec 29 — Rest morning, river and night drive (South Luangwa NP)

Sleep off the travel. The camp sits on the river: hippos, crocodiles, elephants walking through camp, vervets and baboons. Afternoon and night drive.

*Overnight:* Croc Valley Camp · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Wed Dec 30 — First morning drive (South Luangwa NP)

## Options & swaps

- **10 Luangwa nights, home Jan 9** (−$600 pp): Fly Mfuwe → Lusaka on Jan 7 and home on Jan 8. Still 10 night drives. Brings the trip under $5k.
- **Fly into Lilongwe and drive to Mfuwe** (−$250 pp): Lilongwe is ~4.5–5h by road from Mfuwe (via Chipata), and camps run transfers. Replaces the Proflight tickets and Lusaka nights with a road transfer (~$300 per vehicle each way). Needs a Malawi visa ($50), and Malawi's fuel shortage makes the Malawi leg less certain.
- **Kafue: 3 nights at Mukambi instead of 3 in Luangwa** (+$750 pp): Mukambi Safari Lodge is the one camp in central Kafue that stays open in the rains: a different, wilder park with leopard and lion, ~4h by road from Lusaka. ~$380 pp/night full board plus the road transfer.
- **2 nights on Lake Malawi for a dive day** (+$450 pp): Via Lilongwe: Cape Maclear for 2 dives or snorkelling with the cichlids. Costs 2 Luangwa nights, a long road day each way, a Malawi visa, and depends on fuel.
- **Flatdogs all-inclusive instead of Croc Valley pay-per-drive** (+$2,650 pp): $397 pp/night including meals, 2 activities a day, park fees, laundry and transfers.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Zambia:** visa-free for US citizens per 2026 sources (30–90 days; the sources disagree). Passport valid 6+ months with 2 blank pages; carry proof of the onward flight.
- **Yellow fever:** not required arriving from the US; transiting Addis for under 12h doesn't trigger it.

## Packing
- Warm layer for night drives (open vehicles, wind after rain); rain jacket.
- Headtorch each; the camp paths have hippos and elephants at night, so always walk with a guard after dark.
- Binoculars; a fast lens for spotlight work (no flash at animals).
- Neutral colours; insect repellent with DEET; tsetse flies are around, and dark blue and black attract them.

## Money
- US dollars are accepted at the camp; **park fees in cash** (no cards at the gate). Kwacha for small purchases; ATMs in Lusaka and Mfuwe.

## Season
- Late December to January is the wet season: hot (~30–35 °C), humid, heavy afternoon storms. Some interior roads close; the Mfuwe sector loops stay open. Proflight sometimes shifts times in the rains; that's why the plan has a Lusaka night at both ends.

## Health and safety
- Check travel.state.gov for Zambia before booking. The main safety rule here is wildlife: hippos and elephants walk through the Mfuwe camps.
- Malaria is high-risk in the rains: prophylaxis plus long sleeves and repellent at dusk.
- The nearest good hospital is in Lusaka or Johannesburg; evacuation cover is essential.
