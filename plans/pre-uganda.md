# Uganda first, then Kenya's Wild Loop

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=pre-uganda

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-19 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-16 · **Planned cost:** $11,470 per person

## Overview

- **Dates:** LAX **Sat Dec 19** → Uganda → Nairobi → LAX **Sat Jan 16**. Christmas in Uganda; Kenya from Dec 28 is the Wild loop unchanged.
- **Stopover:** LAX→Istanbul (1 night)→Entebbe late Dec 21. Fly to Bwindi gorillas, Christmas in QENP, Kibale chimps, fly Kasese→EBB→Nairobi Dec 27/28. ~+$3,900 pp. US advisory Level 4.
- **Verdict:** Best wildlife stopover: gorillas, chimps, tree-climbing lions, Kazinga hippos in 9 days, ~+$3,900 pp. Deal-breaker: still Level 4 (Jun 4 2026, Ebola) despite outbreak over — book refundable only until Level 3.
- **Safety:** Level 4 – Do Not Travel. Issued Jun 4 2026: crime, Ebola, terrorism, unrest; limited US consular services. Was Level 3 (Dec 8 2025). Not lowered as of Oct 8 2026 despite outbreak declared over (Uganda Jul 28, WHO Aug 26). Re-check travel.state.gov before paying.. Ebola over, but DRC transmission may continue; Bwindi/Ishasha border DRC, one imported case could close parks. ADF (ISIS-linked) killed honeymoon couple + guide in QENP Oct 2023; armed escorts since, no tourist attacks. 2023 Anti-Homosexuality Act applies to visitors; roads deadliest daily risk; real problem is insurance/refunds.
- **Cost:** $11,470 pp, **+$3,870** vs the Wild loop alone ($7,600).

**Highlights:** Bwindi (Buhoma) gorilla trek: 1h with habituated family, ~95%+ success · Kibale chimp tracking: East Africa's densest primates, ~90% success · Ishasha tree-climbing lions on Christmas Eve · Christmas: Kasenyi game drive + Kazinga boat (hippos, buffalo, elephants, birds) · Aerolink light aircraft over green hills; skips 9–10h drives · Free evening/morning in Istanbul · Bigodi Wetland community walk: red colobus, turacos

**Drawbacks:** Level 4 still shown (Jun 4 2026, Ebola); ADF history in QENP; Anti-Homosexuality Act · Priciest option: ~+$3,900 pp (gorilla $800, chimp $250, Aerolink ~$700) · No same-night link: TK10 lands IST ~19:00, IST→EBB departs ~15:20–15:40; IST night, EBB ~22:00 Dec 21 · No Uganda rest day; IST night + slow Dec 22 morning instead; Nairobi rest Dec 28 · Aerolink: no 2026 timetable (2023: Flt 121 EBB 12:30), minimum pax, no KQ connection; late Kasese → KQ419 00:10 · Christmas permits limited (8/family/day). Book Buhoma Dec 23 now or Rushaga/Ruhija; paid in full via operator · Dec 24: ~4.5–5h road (Buhoma→Ishasha→Mweya), at 5h cap · Bwindi trek 2–6+h steep, muddy. Porter $20 · UWA Jul 2026+ tariff unpublished; permits reportedly flat to Jan 1 2027 (chimps → $300); confirm · Rwanda alt: $1,500 permit, 2.5h from Kigali, no flights; similar/higher cost, less wildlife

### Then Kenya (Wild loop)
- **Route (one camping crew, all road):** Nairobi → Ol Pejeta 2n → **Samburu 3n** (NYE) → Thomson's Falls 1n → Lake Nakuru 2n → **Kakamega rainforest 2n** → Kericho 1n → **Mara conservancy 4n** → Naivasha + Hell's Gate 2n → airport hotel → fly home.
- **Exotic wildlife focus:** Samburu's Special Five + vulturine guineafowl, chimps + black rhino (Ol Pejeta), Rothschild's giraffe (Nakuru), colobus + De Brazza's monkeys + great blue turaco (Kakamega), big cats (Mara). See **Wildlife**.
- **No beach, no rental car:** crew 4x4 + driver-guide included.
- **Budget: ~$7,600 pp** (incl. flights + $300 contingency). Crew days are the cost driver.
- **Cuts:** skip Kakamega (−$800), home Jan 14 (−$450), yellow fever done (−$200), fly out of the Mara (−$90).
- **Fatigue:** max ~4.5h road/day, overnight stops split every long leg, 9 zero-travel days. Last day is a ~25h journey home.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 19 — Depart LAX (LAX → Istanbul)

TK10 19:45, ~13h nonstop. Sleep aboard.

Slow morning. TK591 ~15:20, ~6.7h → EBB ~22:00. 15 min to guesthouse.

*Overnight:* Entebbe · *Meals:* B, on board

### Tue Dec 22 — Fly to Bwindi (Entebbe → Bwindi)

Sleep in. Aerolink 12:30, ~1.5h to Kihihi. 1.25h drive, Buhoma ~15:30. Pack for trek.

*Overnight:* Buhoma, Bwindi · *Meals:* B,L,D

### Wed Dec 23 — Gorilla trek (Bwindi (Buhoma))

07:30 briefing. 2–6h steep, muddy trek; 1h with gorillas. Porter $20.

*Overnight:* Buhoma, Bwindi · *Meals:* B,packed L,D

### Thu Dec 24 — Tree-climbing lions (Ishasha → Mweya)

~2h to Ishasha. ~1.5h fig-tree game drive. ~2.5h to Mweya/Katunguru by 17:00. ~4.5–5h road, longest day.

*Overnight:* Queen Elizabeth NP · *Meals:* B,packed L,D

### Fri Dec 25 — Christmas on Kazinga (Queen Elizabeth NP)

Dawn Kasenyi drive ~3h (lions, kob, elephants). Midday rest. 15:00 Kazinga boat 2h. <1h driving.

*Overnight:* Queen Elizabeth NP · *Meals:* B,L,D

### Sat Dec 26 — Chimps (Mweya → Kibale)

~2h via crater lakes. Chimps 14:00, 2–3h. Optional dawn Bigodi walk if flight allows.

*Overnight:* Kibale · *Meals:* B,L,D

### Sun Dec 27 — Fly to Kenya (Kibale → Nairobi)

~2h to Kasese strip. Aerolink ~1.5h to EBB. KQ417 ~18:00 (or KQ419 00:10, lands 01:25 Dec 28). 45 min taxi to Karen. 2h road. Dec 28 rest.

*Overnight:* Nairobi (Karen guesthouse) · *Meals:* B,L

### Mon Dec 28 — Land + rest day (Nairobi)

Wake at the guesthouse (landed last night). Rest. Optional: Sheldrick baby elephants 11:00 ($20, ~20m away; book online now).

*Overnight:* Budget guesthouse, Nairobi · *Meals:* Own

### Tue Dec 29 — Drive north to Ol Pejeta (Nairobi → Ol Pejeta)

Crew pickup ~08:30. ~4h via Nanyuki, equator stop. Camp set at OPC campsite. PM: chimp sanctuary + sunset drive.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, OPC campsite · *Meals:* L, D camp

### Wed Dec 30 — Rhinos + Mt Kenya (Ol Pejeta Conservancy)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives. Black/white rhino, lion, elephant, Grevy's zebra. Optional lion tracking (~$70; not budgeted).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, OPC campsite · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Dec 31 — North to Samburu + NYE by the river (Ol Pejeta → Samburu)

~2.5–3h via Nanyuki + Isiolo; drop into hot, dry north. Camp on the Ewaso Ng'iro river. PM drive. Quiet NYE under the stars.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu/Buffalo Springs campsite · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 01 — Samburu Special Five (Samburu National Reserve)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, Beisa oryx, Somali ostrich. Vulturine guineafowl, river elephants. Midday siesta (30–35°C).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sat Jan 02 — Leopards + river day (Samburu National Reserve)

Zero travel. Search riverine forest for leopard; lions, cheetah possible. Optional Samburu village visit (~$20–30 pp, paid locally).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sun Jan 03 — Back to the cool highlands (Samburu → Nyahururu)

~3.5h via Isiolo + Nanyuki. Thomson's Falls (74 m) walk to the bottom. Camp at the falls; cool night (fleece).

*Overnight:* Thomson's Falls campsite, Nyahururu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Mon Jan 04 — Short hop to Nakuru (Nyahururu → Lake Nakuru)

~1.5h downhill to Lake Nakuru NP. Camp inside the park. PM drive: rhino, Rothschild's giraffe.

*Overnight:* KWS campsite, Lake Nakuru NP · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Tue Jan 05 — Nakuru day (Lake Nakuru NP)

Zero travel. Dawn drive to Baboon Cliff. Black + white rhino, flamingos/pelicans (numbers vary), tree-climbing lions if lucky. Siesta.

*Overnight:* KWS campsite, Lake Nakuru NP · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Wed Jan 06 — West to the rainforest (Lake Nakuru → Kakamega Forest)

~4–4.5h via Eldoret. Rondo Retreat inside the forest, full board. Dusk walk: colobus calling.

*Overnight:* Rondo Retreat, Kakamega Forest · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Jan 07 — Rainforest day (Kakamega Forest)

Zero travel. Dawn bird walk with local guide (~Ksh300–500 pp): great blue turaco, hornbills. Black-and-white colobus, De Brazza's + red-tailed monkeys. Lirhanda Hill. Optional night walk (pottos, bushbabies).

*Overnight:* Rondo Retreat, Kakamega Forest · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 08 — Tea country (Kakamega → Kericho)

~2.5–3h via Kisumu. Tea-estate walk, factory visit. Easy afternoon.

*Overnight:* Budget hotel, Kericho · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 09 — Into the Mara conservancy (Kericho → Masai Mara)

~3–3.5h via Bomet + Mulot (last hour rough). Camp in Enonkishu (or Pardamat/Ol Chorro). Dusk drive.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sun Jan 10 — Full Mara day (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives, off-road, few vehicles. Big cats, plains game, newborn topi. Siesta.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Mon Jan 11 — Maasai walk + night drive (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Morning walk with Maasai ranger. PM drive. Night drive if allowed (aardvark, bat-eared fox, genets).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Tue Jan 12 — Extra Mara day (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Full-day drive with picnic lunch, following whatever the guides found. Sundowner.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Wed Jan 13 — Road to Naivasha (Mara → Lake Naivasha)

~4–4.5h via Narok + Mai Mahiu. Lakeshore camp. Hippos at dusk.

*Overnight:* Lakeshore campsite, Naivasha · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Jan 14 — Hell's Gate + boat (Lake Naivasha / Hell's Gate)

No long travel (30m local). Bike or walk Hell's Gate among zebra + giraffe, gorge hike. PM boat + Crescent Island walk. Rest.

*Overnight:* Lakeshore campsite, Naivasha · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 15 — Airport hotel (Naivasha → Nairobi (JKIA area))

~2.5–3h via A104 + Southern Bypass. Tip crew. Shower, repack, early night.

*Overnight:* Budget hotel near JKIA · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 16 — Fly home (Nairobi → Istanbul → Los Angeles)

Wake ~02:45, 10–20m to JKIA. TK608 04:50 → IST 11:45. ~2h45 airside connect. TK9 ~14:30 → LAX ~18:15 same day. ~25h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Already have yellow fever shot** (−$200 pp): Skip the $200 vaccine. Bring your yellow card.
- **Skip Kakamega + Kericho; home Jan 13** (−$800 pp): Nakuru → Mara direct (~4.5–5h). Loses the rainforest primates + turacos; 3 days shorter.
- **Come home Jan 14 instead of Jan 16** (−$450 pp): Drop the extra Mara day + Hell's Gate day. Same loop otherwise.
- **Ol Pejeta specials: northern white rhino + lion tracking** (+$140 pp): Visit the last northern white rhino(s) with their keepers (~$70; check status before booking) + lion tracking with researchers (~$70).
- **Fly Mara → Wilson; Nairobi instead of Naivasha** (−$90 pp): ~2.5h door to door vs 4–4.5h drive. Drops Naivasha + Hell's Gate; 2 Nairobi hotel nights instead (Sheldrick, Giraffe Centre possible).
- **Fly Nairobi → Nanyuki for Ol Pejeta** (+$204 pp): ~2.5h door to door vs ~4h drive. Crew drives up empty anyway.
- **Tented-camp comfort at Ol Pejeta + Samburu** (+$650 pp): Sweetwaters Serena 2n + Samburu Riverside Camp 3n, full board; crew keeps vehicle + guide.
