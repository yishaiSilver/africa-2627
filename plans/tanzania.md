# Istanbul & Tanzania: Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Mikumi and the Udzungwa rainforest

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=tanzania

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $5,455 per person

## Overview

- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 17 nights: 3 Istanbul, 13 Tanzania, 1 on a plane.
- **Route:** Istanbul 3n → Arusha 1n → **6-day northern camping safari** (Tarangire, Serengeti 3n, Ngorongoro rim, crater) → Arusha 1n → fly to Dar → **4-day Mikumi + Udzungwa** → home from Dar.
- **Why Istanbul Dec 30, not Dec 31:** one fewer paid hotel night and day of food (~$95 pp), and the trip ends a day sooner. Istanbul is on Tanzania time, so the jet lag is handled there; the Dec 31 Arusha day only has to absorb the 01:40 landing.
- **Why Udzungwa and Mikumi:** Udzungwa is real Eastern Arc rainforest with 12 primate species, two of them found nowhere else (Udzungwa red colobus, Sanje mangabey). Mikumi is the savanna next door: a second, quiet big-game park.
- **Big 5:** lion, elephant, buffalo near-certain; leopard gets three Serengeti days plus Mikumi; rhino rests on the Ngorongoro crater.
- **Budget: ~$5,455 pp,** just under the $5.5k ceiling, with **no contingency**. To fit, the northern safari is 6 days (Lake Manyara cut) and Istanbul ends Dec 30. Manyara is one toggle away in Options.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

TK10 LAX 19:45 → IST 19:00 next day (~13h). Istanbul is on Tanzania time (UTC+3), so the jet-lag shift is done once, here.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land Istanbul, metro into the city (Istanbul)

Land 19:00. Metro M11 → M2 → T1 to Sultanahmet, ~1.5h door to door. Buy an Istanbulkart at the station. Easy dinner, early night.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Mon Dec 28 — Sultanahmet on foot (Istanbul)

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern — all within ten minutes of each other. Zero travel.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Tue Dec 29 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus (Istanbul)

Topkapı in the morning, Grand Bazaar after lunch, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for dinner.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Free morning, fly to Kilimanjaro (Istanbul → in flight)

Spice Bazaar or Süleymaniye. Bags at the hotel. Leave for the airport by 15:00. TK Istanbul 18:30 → Kilimanjaro 01:40 (summer 2026 timing; confirm the Dec schedule).

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B; in-flight

### Thu Dec 31 — Land 01:40, rest day in Arusha (Kilimanjaro → Arusha)

Visa check at JRO, pre-booked taxi to Arusha (~1h). Room held from Dec 30 so it's ready at 03:00. Sleep in, then a slow day: Tanzanian SIM, cash, meet the safari operator for the briefing. Quiet New Year's Eve.

*Overnight:* Arusha budget hotel · *Meals:* Own

### Fri Jan 01 — Safari day 1: Tarangire elephants (Arusha → Tarangire)

Pickup ~08:00, ~2.5h to Tarangire. Full afternoon drive: big elephant herds, baobabs, lion. Camp near Mto wa Mbu or Karatu.

*Overnight:* Safari camp (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Sat Jan 02 — Safari day 2: into the Serengeti (Ngorongoro highlands → Serengeti)

Up over the Ngorongoro highlands (rim forest views), down through Ngorongoro conservation area to Naabi Hill gate, game drive to Seronera. ~6–7h with driving and drives. In January the herds are on the southern short-grass plains, so you may cross them on the way in.

*Overnight:* Seronera public campsite · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Sun Jan 03 — Safari day 3: leopard day (Serengeti (Seronera))

Zero transfer. Dawn to dusk in the Seronera valley: the sausage trees and kopjes are the best leopard country on the circuit. Lion and cheetah are routine here.

*Overnight:* Seronera public campsite · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D safari

### Mon Jan 04 — Safari day 4: the herds or more cats (Serengeti)

Zero transfer. Ask the guide on day 2 whether a run toward the southern plains / Ndutu edge is worth it this year; otherwise a second full Seronera day for leopard.

*Overnight:* Seronera public campsite · *Meals:* B, L (packed), D safari

### Tue Jan 05 — Safari day 5: last drive, up to the rim (Serengeti → Ngorongoro rim)

Morning drive, then ~3h to Simba campsite on the crater rim (2,300 m — cold; buffalo and elephant wander through camp at night).

*Overnight:* Simba campsite, Ngorongoro rim · *Meals:* B, L, D safari

### Wed Jan 06 — Safari day 6: crater floor for rhino (Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha)

Descend at gate opening (~06:00) for the full 6h crater allowance: black rhino (the best chance on the trip), lion, hyena, flamingo. Out by midday, ~4h back to Arusha.

*Overnight:* Arusha budget hotel · *Meals:* B, L safari; D own

### Thu Jan 07 — Fly south to Dar (Arusha → Dar es Salaam)

Precision Air or Air Tanzania, Arusha (ARK) or Kilimanjaro (JRO) → Dar, ~1h15 direct. Afternoon off; laundry. Meet the Mikumi–Udzungwa operator.

*Overnight:* Dar es Salaam budget hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Fri Jan 08 — South trip day 1: Mikumi (Dar → Mikumi)

Operator pickup. SGR train to Morogoro (~1.5h) then road to Mikumi, or ~5h by road. Afternoon game drive: elephant, giraffe, buffalo, hippo pools, lion.

*Overnight:* Lodge/camp near Mikumi (in package) · *Meals:* L, D tour

### Sat Jan 09 — South trip day 2: full day in Mikumi (Mikumi NP)

Zero transfer. Full-day drive on the Mkata floodplain; leopard is possible in the wooded hills. Few vehicles compared with the north.

*Overnight:* Lodge/camp near Mikumi (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D tour

### Sun Jan 10 — South trip day 3: rainforest on foot (Udzungwa Mountains NP)

~1.5h to Mang'ula. Full day hiking with a park guide: Sanje Falls trail (170 m falls, ~5–6h), with a primate-focused walk for the Udzungwa (Iringa) red colobus and Sanje crested mangabey. Chameleons, forest birds. Udzungwa is walking-only — no vehicles inside.

*Overnight:* Guesthouse/camp at Mang'ula (in package) · *Meals:* B, L, D tour

### Mon Jan 11 — Back to Dar (Udzungwa → Dar es Salaam)

Early start, road to Morogoro (~3h) and SGR or road back to Dar. Hotel near the airport with the room until midnight; shower, repack, sleep a few hours.

*Overnight:* Dar hotel near the airport (until ~midnight) · *Meals:* B tour; L, D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Fly home (Dar es Salaam → Los Angeles)

Leave the hotel ~00:30. TK604 DAR ~03:25 → IST ~11:05, then TK9 → LAX, arriving the same evening. About 27h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **7-day safari: add a Lake Manyara day** (+$250 pp): Groundwater forest with blue monkeys, baboons and elephants on the way back. Puts the trip ~$200 over the ceiling.
- **Stay in Istanbul until Dec 31** (+$95 pp): A fourth night (paid) and another day of food. Fare difference unknown until you price it; flying on Dec 31 is sometimes cheaper.
- **Second Udzungwa day** (+$200 pp): The red colobus and mangabey trails are different from the Sanje Falls route. One more lodge night, guide and park fee.
- **Arusha National Park day** (+$180 pp): Forest with black-and-white colobus, buffalo, giraffe, and walking safaris with an armed ranger. Fits on Dec 31 only if you skip the rest day.
- **Buffer day in Dar before the flight** (+$55 pp): Avoids the Udzungwa → Dar → 03:25 flight in one stretch. Home Jan 13.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Tanzania:** US citizens need the $100 multiple-entry visa. Apply only at visa.immigration.go.tz; lookalike sites overcharge. Passport 6+ months, 2 blank pages.
- **Yellow fever:** not required arriving from Istanbul. Tanzania asks for it only after >12h transit in a risk country (Kenya counts — don't route via Nairobi).
- Türkiye is visa-free for US tourists.

## Packing
- One soft bag each; the Arusha → Dar flight has tight bag limits.
- **Warm layers for the crater rim** (2,300 m, near freezing at night) and dawn drives. Istanbul is 4–9°C and wet.
- Hiking shoes with grip for the Sanje Falls trail; it's steep and muddy in places. Headtorch for camping.
- Neutral colours; tsetse flies in Tarangire and parts of the Serengeti go for dark blue and black.
- Plastic bags are banned in Tanzania, including in luggage.

## Money
- Park fees are cashless and paid by the operator. Carry crisp post-2013 USD for tips and the visa backup; TZS for meals and taxis.

## Season
- Early January is the short dry spell between rains. Wildebeest herds are on the southern Serengeti / Ndutu plains, with calving from late January.

## Health
- Malaria prophylaxis from before arrival through 7 days after (atovaquone-proguanil). DEET or picaridin.
- Re-check the State Dept Tanzania advisory before paying deposits.
