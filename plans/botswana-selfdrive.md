# Botswana self-drive: Nxai Pan, the Central Kalahari, Khama rhinos, Moremi, Khwai, Savuti and Chobe, then Victoria Falls and Istanbul

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=botswana-selfdrive

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-27 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-16 · **Planned cost:** $6,215 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** a camping self-drive through Botswana in the green season, built to see as many of the famous animals as possible: **lions, cheetah, leopard, elephants, hippos, crocodiles, buffalo, giraffe and rhino**. Then a night at **Victoria Falls** and **2 nights in Istanbul** on the way home.
- **Dates:** LAX Sun Dec 27 → LAX Sat Jan 16. 13 nights in Botswana (11 camping, 2 in Maun), 1 at Victoria Falls, 1 Johannesburg airport hotel, 2 Istanbul, plus 3 on planes.
- **Route (one-way, Maun → Kasane):** ✈ via Istanbul to Johannesburg (airport hotel) → ✈ Maun 1n → **Nxai Pan 2n** → **CKGR Deception Valley 2n** → **Khama Rhino Sanctuary 1n** → Maun 1n (restock, fill every tank) → **Moremi: Third Bridge 1n, Xakanaxa 1n** → **Khwai 2n** → **Savuti 1n** → **Chobe Riverfront (Ihaha) 1n** → return the 4x4 in Kasane → **Victoria Falls 1n** → ✈ Johannesburg → ✈ **Istanbul 2n** → home.
- **The vehicle:** Avis Safari Hilux Double Cab Bushcamper with **two rooftop tents** (one each), big fridge, hot water and solar, with Super Cover (zero excess).
- **⛽ Fuel is the critical item:** no fuel in any park, and none between Maun and Kasane: ~800–900 km over 7 days, mostly deep sand. Carry **≥160 L** (the 150 L tank plus a 20 L jerry can), fill everything in Maun on Jan 5, and top up at Rakops and Letlhakane on the Kalahari leg.
- **Cats:** Kalahari lions and cheetah at Nxai Pan and Deception; leopard best at Khwai (guided morning and night drives) and Moremi; Savuti's lions; wild dog possible at Khwai and Savuti.
- **Rhino:** Khama Rhino Sanctuary near Serowe: white rhino almost certain, black rhino possible.
- **Elephants, hippos, crocodiles:** elephant bulls at Nxai Pan, herds at Khwai, Savuti and Chobe; hippos and crocodiles at Third Bridge, Xakanaxa (boat trip), Khwai, the Chobe riverfront and the Zambezi sunset cruise.
- **Season:** El Niño is forecast to make this a drier-than-normal rainy season, which concentrates animals along the rivers and waterholes but may make the Kalahari greening patchier. Hot (32–40 °C), with afternoon storms.
- **The catch:** you drive, cook and navigate yourselves, on sand and black-clay mud; no self-driving after dark in the parks. Book every campsite before collecting the car.
- **Budget: ~$6,215 pp** (≈ $12,430 for two). Flights ~$2,410 pp, the vehicle ~$1,365 pp including the one-way fee.

## Crowds and wildlife

1–5, for late December and early January. Crowds: 1 = you rarely see another vehicle, 5 = queues of vehicles at sightings (lower is better). Wildlife numbers: how many animals you see. Wildlife diversity: how many different kinds. These are judgments from how each place works in this season, not counts.

| Stop | Nights | Crowds | Numbers | Diversity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nxai Pan | 2 | 1/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | A handful of vehicles at the main waterhole at most. Zebra and springbok on the grassland, lions and cheetah following; elephant bulls at the waterhole. |
| Central Kalahari: Deception Valley | 2 | 1/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | Often no other vehicle at your pan. Springbok and gemsbok herds with lions and cheetah. No hippos, few elephants. |
| Khama Rhino Sanctuary | 1 | 2/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | Small fenced sanctuary: white rhino almost guaranteed, black rhino possible, with giraffe, zebra and eland. |

| Chobe Riverfront | 1 | 3/5 | 4/5 | 4/5 | Elephants, buffalo, hippos and crocodiles along the river; fewer elephants than in the dry season. |

| **Whole trip** | | 2/5 | 4.5/5 | 5/5 | All of the Big Five plus hippos, crocodiles and giraffe, and Victoria Falls. Empty in the Kalahari; Khwai and the Chobe riverfront are busier. You'll see a smaller share of the cats than on a guided trip, because nobody is tracking them for you. |

**Pros**

- All of the Big Five, including rhino at Khama, plus hippos, crocodiles and giraffe.
- Whole pans to yourselves in the Kalahari.
- Victoria Falls added without losing a safari day.
- Your own pace, one tent each, and well under the price of a guided trip.

**Cons**

- You drive, navigate, camp and cook; long sand days and the risk of getting stuck alone.
- Fuel planning is critical: no fuel for ~800–900 km between Maun and Kasane.
- Fewer leopard sightings without a guide, except the guided drives at Khwai.
- Holiday-season campsites may be full; a one-way rental fee.
- Hot (32–40 °C) with thunderstorms.

## Itinerary

### Sun Dec 27 — Fly to Istanbul (Los Angeles → in flight)

Turkish from LAX at 18:30 (~13h). One Turkish multi-city ticket covers LAX → Istanbul → Johannesburg, Johannesburg → Istanbul and Istanbul → LAX (~$1,910 pp on Google Flights, Nov 22). Choose a fare that allows changes.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Mon Dec 28 — Connect in Istanbul (Istanbul → in flight)

Land in Istanbul in the evening; ~7h layover airside, then the overnight flight to Johannesburg. Sleep on both flights if you can.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Tue Dec 29 — Land in Johannesburg, airport hotel (Johannesburg)

Land 11:00. The Maun flights leave at 11:25 and 12:05, too soon to connect, so stay one night at an airport hotel connected to the terminal by a walkway (InterContinental or City Lodge O.R. Tambo). Rest, or take an Uber in daylight to the Apartheid Museum (closes 17:00). Buy a SIM and some rand at the airport. Don't walk around Johannesburg after dark.

*Overnight:* Johannesburg airport hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; L, D own

### Wed Dec 30 — Fly to Maun, collect the 4x4 (Johannesburg → Maun)

Airlink 12:05 → 13:45 (or CemAir 11:25 → 13:45). Collect the Avis Safari Hilux Double Cab Bushcamper (2 rooftop tents) at ~15:00: check both tents, the fridge, the tyres and the fuel capacity, and add a 20 L jerry can. Shop for 5 days of food (Dec 31 – Jan 4) and 80+ litres of water; fill the tank. Shops close for the Jan 1–2 public holidays, but you'll be in the parks. Botswana is UTC+2.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Maun · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Thu Dec 31 — Into Nxai Pan (Maun → Nxai Pan)

~3h: tar to the turn-off, then 35 km of deep sand (tyres to ~1.6 bar). Afternoon at the main waterhole: elephant bulls, springbok, zebra, and lions and cheetah on the green grassland. New Year's Eve in the bush.

*Overnight:* Nxai Pan South Camp · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Fri Jan 01 — Nxai Pan: dawn and dusk on the pan (Nxai Pan)

Zero transfer. Dawn and late-afternoon drives on the pan and around the waterhole; midday in the shade at camp. Optional run to Baines' Baobabs (~1h each way).

*Overnight:* Nxai Pan South Camp · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Sat Jan 02 — To the Central Kalahari (Nxai Pan → Central Kalahari (Deception Valley))

~6h: back to the tar, west to Motopi, south to Rakops (⛽ last fuel before the CKGR: fill the tank and the jerry can), then Matswere gate and Deception Valley. Afternoon drive in the valley.

*Overnight:* Deception Valley campsite (CKGR) · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Sun Jan 03 — Deception Valley: lions, cheetah, springbok herds (CKGR: Deception Valley)

Zero transfer. Dawn and dusk loops to Sunday Pan, Leopard Pan and Passarge Valley. Green-season herds of springbok and gemsbok draw the Kalahari's black-maned lions and cheetah.

*Overnight:* Deception Valley campsite (CKGR) · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Mon Jan 04 — Out of the Kalahari to the rhinos (Deception Valley → Khama Rhino Sanctuary)

Dawn drive, then out at Matswere by mid-morning. Rakops and Letlhakane (⛽ fuel and shops), then Serowe to the sanctuary (~4–5h). Afternoon guided drive or self-drive around the pans and waterholes: white rhino almost certain, black rhino possible, plus giraffe, zebra and eland.

*Overnight:* Khama Rhino Sanctuary campsite · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Tue Jan 05 — Rhinos at dawn, then restock in Maun (Khama Rhino Sanctuary → Maun)

Dawn rhino drive, then ~5–6h to Maun via Letlhakane, Rakops and Motopi. **⛽ Fill the tank and all jerry cans to the brim: there is no fuel between Maun and Kasane (~800–900 km, mostly deep sand).** Restock 6 days of food and 80+ litres of water, do laundry, check the tyres.

*Overnight:* Lodge in Maun · *Meals:* Self-catering; D own

### Wed Jan 06 — Into Moremi: Third Bridge (Maun → Moremi (Third Bridge))

~2h to South Gate, then ~1.5h to Third Bridge (ask at South Gate which tracks are open after the floods; if Third Bridge is closed, spend both Moremi nights at Xakanaxa). Afternoon on the Mboma loop: lion, leopard, hippos, crocodiles, red lechwe, elephants.

*Overnight:* Third Bridge campsite, Moremi · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Thu Jan 07 — Moremi lagoons and a boat trip (Third Bridge → Xakanaxa)

Dawn drive, then slowly via Fourth Bridge and the Dombo hippo pool to Xakanaxa (~1.5h without stops). Afternoon boat trip on the lagoon for hippos and crocodiles up close (check it's running at January water levels; Mboma is the backup).

*Overnight:* Xakanaxa campsite, Moremi · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Fri Jan 08 — Dawn drive, then Khwai (Xakanaxa → Khwai)

Dawn drive at Xakanaxa, then ~1.5–2h east via North Gate into the Khwai community area. Afternoon along the Khwai River: elephants, hippos, leopard, wild dog.

*Overnight:* Khwai (Magotho) community campsite · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Sat Jan 09 — Khwai: guided drives for leopard and wild dog (Khwai)

Zero transfer. A Khwai community guide for the morning (off-road driving is allowed here) and a guided night drive in the evening: the best chance at leopard, plus genets, servals and owls.

*Overnight:* Khwai (Magotho) community campsite · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Sun Jan 10 — To Savuti (Khwai → Savuti (Chobe NP))

~3–4h via Mababe gate on deep sand. Ask the Khwai guides that morning whether the Marsh road or the Sand Ridge road is better (black-clay mud after rain). Afternoon on the Savuti Marsh and channel: lions, zebra on their green-season migration, elephants. Fuel check: you should still have well over half.

*Overnight:* Savuti campsite · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Mon Jan 11 — North to the Chobe River (Savuti → Chobe Riverfront)

Dawn drive at Savuti, then north via Ghoha Hills gate, Kachikau and Ngoma (~4–5h), entering the riverfront from the west. Afternoon drive: elephant herds, buffalo, hippos, crocodiles, puku, sable.

*Overnight:* Ihaha campsite, Chobe Riverfront · *Meals:* Self-catering

### Tue Jan 12 — Last game drive, then Victoria Falls (Chobe Riverfront → Kasane → Victoria Falls)

Dawn drive east along the riverfront to Sedudu gate. ⛽ Refuel in Kasane, clean out the vehicle and return it at the Kasane depot ~12:00. Transfer to Victoria Falls (~1.5h with the Kazungula border; Zimbabwe visa on arrival). Afternoon walk through the rainforest along the falls, then a Zambezi sunset cruise: hippos, crocodiles, often elephants.

*Overnight:* Victoria Falls hotel · *Meals:* Self-catering; D own

### Wed Jan 13 — Falls in the morning, fly to Johannesburg (Victoria Falls → Johannesburg)

Morning back at the falls (or the bridge, or a rest by the pool). At the airport by ~12:00 for Airlink 14:00 → 15:45 or South African 14:20 → 16:05. In Johannesburg, check in for Turkish and wait airside (dinner, or a day room) for the 00:20 departure.

*Overnight:* Plane (departs 00:20 Jan 14) · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Thu Jan 14 — Land in Istanbul: the old city (Johannesburg → Istanbul)

TK243 00:20 → Istanbul 11:25. Airport metro (M11 → M2 → T1) to Sultanahmet (~1.5h) or a taxi. Drop bags, then Hagia Sophia, the Basilica Cistern, the Blue Mosque and the Hippodrome, all within ten minutes' walk.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; L, D own

### Fri Jan 15 — Topkapı, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus ferry (Istanbul)

Topkapı Palace at opening, the Grand Bazaar and the Spice Bazaar, then a public ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for the food market and dinner.

*Overnight:* Istanbul hotel · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 16 — Fly home (Istanbul → Los Angeles)

Free morning (Süleymaniye Mosque, or coffee by the Galata Bridge). Turkish 13:50 → LAX 16:35 the same day (an 08:00 → 10:50 flight also runs).

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* B; in-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Okavango scenic flight from Maun** (+$150 pp): 45–60 min over the Delta in a small plane on the afternoon of the Jan 5 restock day.
- **Maun loop instead of Kasane (if Savuti is full or the one-way fee is high)** (−$350 pp): Skip Savuti, Chobe and Victoria Falls: 3 nights each in Moremi and Khwai, return the car in Maun and fly Maun → Johannesburg on Jan 13.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Botswana:** no visa for US citizens (up to 90 days). Pay the $30 Tourism Development Levy online beforehand. Passport valid 6+ months with 2 blank pages.
- **South Africa (transit):** visa-free for US citizens; you clear immigration at Johannesburg on Dec 29 and again for the Jan 13 connection.
- **Zimbabwe (Victoria Falls):** US citizens get a visa on arrival at Kazungula (~$30 single entry; check the current fee). Carry US dollars in small notes.
- **Türkiye:** visa-free for US citizens (90 days).

## Johannesburg
- Stay at a hotel connected to the terminal; you never leave the airport precinct. Go out only in daylight, by Uber or hotel car, with phones and cameras out of sight. Avoid the inner city (CBD, Hillbrow).

## Driving
- **Left-hand traffic.** Both drivers need a US licence; an International Driving Permit is advisable.
- Deflate to ~1.6 bar in sand; re-inflate on the tar. Never drive the parks after dark.
- **Black-clay mud** on the CKGR pans, around Nxai and on the Mababe–Savuti track after rain: stay on the tracks and turn back rather than get stuck alone.
- **⛽ Fuel:** Maun, Rakops (last before the CKGR), Letlhakane and Kasane only. **Nothing between Maun (Jan 5) and Kasane (Jan 12):** ~800–900 km, mostly deep sand, at ~15–18 L/100 km. Carry ≥160 L, fill everything in Maun and track consumption daily. If you're below half at Khwai, skip detours and head for Kasane.
- Carry 20+ litres of water each beyond drinking needs, and the satellite phone. Tell the rental firm your route.

## Camping
- Unfenced sites: lions, hyenas and elephants walk through. Sleep in the rooftop tents, zip up, never walk away from the car after dark, and keep food in the vehicle.
- No showers at Deception Valley; Khwai's are unreliable. Ask the rental firm for a camp shower.
- Charge phones and camera batteries while driving (USB/12V); don't drain the fridge battery overnight. Mains power in Maun, Khama, Victoria Falls.
- Bring firewood from Maun; collecting it in the parks isn't allowed.

## Food
- Big shops in Maun on Dec 30 (5 days) and Jan 5 (6 days); top-up at Letlhakane on Jan 4. Fresh meat for 3–4 days, then tins, pasta, rice, eggs, long-life milk.

## Money
- Botswana pula; cards work in Maun and Kasane. Carry pula for gates, firewood and tips, and US dollars for Zimbabwe.

## Season
- Late December–January: hot (32–40 °C), afternoon thunderstorms. A drier El Niño year is forecast, which concentrates animals near permanent water.

## Health and safety
- Malaria risk in Moremi, Khwai, Chobe and Victoria Falls: take prophylaxis. The nearest hospitals are in Maun and Kasane; evacuation cover is essential.

## Istanbul in January
- Cold and often wet (~4–9 °C): pack a warm layer and a rain jacket, which you'll also want on Kalahari dawn drives.


## Open questions

1. Do the campsites confirm? Savuti and Nxai Pan over New Year are the hardest to replace.
2. What is the one-way fee to Kasane? If it's high, the Maun loop saves money but drops Savuti, Chobe and Victoria Falls.
3. Is Third Bridge open after the 2026 floods? If not, 2 nights at Xakanaxa.
