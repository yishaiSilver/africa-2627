# Morocco first, then Kenya's Wild Loop

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=pre-morocco

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-19 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-16 · **Planned cost:** $9,595 per person

## Overview

- **Dates:** LAX **Sat Dec 19** → Morocco → Nairobi → LAX **Sat Jan 16**. Christmas in Morocco; Kenya from Dec 28 is the Wild loop unchanged.
- **Stopover:** 7 nights: Marrakech, High Atlas, Erg Chebbi dunes (Christmas Eve camp), Middle Atlas cedars for Barbary macaques. Out CMN via Cairo; land NBO ~04:55 Dec 28.
- **Verdict:** Big culture/scenery change before Kenya; wildlife thin beyond macaques, camels, birds. Costs: long Dec 27 via Cairo (verify MS849 23:55 not 20:55), freezing desert nights, ~+$1,995 pp.
- **Safety:** US State Dept Level 2: Exercise Increased Caution (terrorism). Advisory reissued Sep 4 2026, level unchanged.. Stable, tourist-friendly; Level 2 for general terrorism (tourist sites, transport, markets; last deadly attacks 2011 Marrakech, 2018 Imlil). Daily risks: petty theft, medina hustlers, winter mountain roads. Merzouga near closed Algerian border, but dune zone safe.
- **Cost:** $9,595 pp, **+$1,995** vs the Wild loop alone ($7,600).

**Highlights:** Christmas Eve Erg Chebbi camp: sunset camels, stars, Berber drums, sunrise on 150 m dunes · Barbary macaques, Cedre Gouraud forest near Azrou (Africa's only macaque, endangered) · High Atlas via Tizi n'Tichka to UNESCO ksar Ait Benhaddou · Todra Gorge (300 m walls), Dades 'road of a thousand kasbahs' · Marrakech medina, Jemaa el-Fna night market, Majorelle + Berber Museum · Merzouga/Dayet Srji birding: desert sparrow, fulvous babbler, flamingos if wet · Khamlia Gnawa music on Christmas Day

**Drawbacks:** Wildlife weak vs Kenya: macaques only real mammal; fennec, Barbary sheep rare · No Morocco-NBO nonstop. MS848 CMN 13:30-CAI 20:25, then MS849; only 23:55 slot connects (20:55 seen in 2026). Verify · Dec 26 (~5h road) + Dec 27 (~3.5h road + ~13h flights/layover) back to back; Dec 28 rest absorbs · Dec 19 is Sat; AT251 LAX-CMN (10:20) flies Sun/Tue/Fri only, so connect via Europe or leave Fri Dec 18. Turkish forces Istanbul overnight · Cold desert/Middle Atlas nights; budget riads, auberges poorly heated · Peak European holiday season: book camps, riads now · Upgrade: luxury camp ($180-350 pp/night) + private 4x4 guide, ~+$400-700 pp · Cheaper: shared 3-day Marrakech-Merzouga-Fes tours (~$190-300 pp), but 9-10h drives break pace rule

### Then Kenya (Wild loop)
- **Route (one camping crew, all road):** Nairobi → Ol Pejeta 2n → **Samburu 3n** (NYE) → Thomson's Falls 1n → Lake Nakuru 2n → **Kakamega rainforest 2n** → Kericho 1n → **Mara conservancy 4n** → Naivasha + Hell's Gate 2n → airport hotel → fly home.
- **Exotic wildlife focus:** Samburu's Special Five + vulturine guineafowl, chimps + black rhino (Ol Pejeta), Rothschild's giraffe (Nakuru), colobus + De Brazza's monkeys + great blue turaco (Kakamega), big cats (Mara). See **Wildlife**.
- **No beach, no rental car:** crew 4x4 + driver-guide included.
- **Budget: ~$7,600 pp** (incl. flights + $300 contingency). Crew days are the cost driver.
- **Cuts:** skip Kakamega (−$800), home Jan 14 (−$450), yellow fever done (−$200), fly out of the Mara (−$90).
- **Fatigue:** max ~4.5h road/day, overnight stops split every long leg, 9 zero-travel days. Last day is a ~25h journey home.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 19 — Depart LAX (LAX to Paris)

Afternoon LAX-CDG (~10.5h, overnight). Sleep on plane.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 20 — Arrive Marrakech (Paris to Marrakech)

Land CDG ~10-11:30, connect to RAK (~3.5h), arr late afternoon. Riad pickup ~25 min. Mint tea, early night. ~6h travel + yesterday's 10.5h.

*Overnight:* Marrakech · *Meals:* In-flight / riad dinner

### Mon Dec 21 — Rest + medina (Marrakech)

No transport. Slow morning. Majorelle + Berber Museum, souks, Ben Youssef Medersa. Sunset terrace over Jemaa el-Fna, night food stalls. Driver briefing.

*Overnight:* Marrakech · *Meals:* B riad, L/D medina

### Tue Dec 22 — Over the Atlas (High Atlas)

Driver 08:30. Tizi n'Tichka, ~4h with stops. Afternoon Ait Benhaddou ksar walk, granary at sunset. Moussier's redstart on pass.

*Overnight:* Ait Benhaddou · *Meals:* B, L roadside tagine, D guesthouse (half board)

### Wed Dec 23 — Kasbah valley (Dades + Todra)

~4h: Ouarzazate, Skoura, Dades switchbacks, Todra. Late-afternoon gorge walk (300 m walls).

*Overnight:* Todra Gorge · *Meals:* B, L Dades, D auberge (HB)

### Thu Dec 24 — Christmas Eve, dunes (Erg Chebbi)

Morning gorge walk, ~3h road to Merzouga. ~15:30 camel 1-1.5h to camp; sunset, dinner, drums, stars. Cold (1-5 C).

*Overnight:* Erg Chebbi desert camp · *Meals:* B, L Rissani/Merzouga, D camp

### Fri Dec 25 — Christmas rest (Merzouga)

Dune sunrise, camel back ~1h, breakfast + shower at auberge. Lazy. Optional: Khamlia Gnawa, Dayet Srji birding, nap.

*Overnight:* Merzouga · *Meals:* B camp, L/D auberge (HB)

### Sat Dec 26 — Cedars + macaques (Middle Atlas)

Dep 08:00. ~5h: Ziz Gorges, Midelt lunch, Azrou. ~15:00 Cedre Gouraud macaques; Levaillant's woodpecker possible. Snow possible.

*Overnight:* Azrou / Ifrane · *Meals:* B, L Midelt, D hotel

### Sun Dec 27 — Long haul to Kenya (CMN-CAI-NBO)

Leave 07:30, ~3.5h (~300 km) to CMN. MS848 13:30-CAI 20:25, ~3.5h airside, MS849 23:55-NBO 04:55 Dec 28. Then Nairobi rest day.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B hotel, in-flight

### Mon Dec 28 — Land + rest day (Nairobi)

Land early morning (see Route). Taxi (~20m) to guesthouse (room held from Dec 27). Sleep. Optional: Sheldrick baby elephants 11:00 ($20, ~20m away; book online now).

*Overnight:* Budget guesthouse, Nairobi · *Meals:* Own

### Tue Dec 29 — Drive north to Ol Pejeta (Nairobi → Ol Pejeta)

Crew pickup ~08:30. ~4h via Nanyuki, equator stop. Camp set at OPC campsite. PM: chimp sanctuary + sunset drive.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, OPC campsite · *Meals:* L, D camp

### Wed Dec 30 — Rhinos + Mt Kenya (Ol Pejeta Conservancy)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives. Black/white rhino, lion, elephant, Grevy's zebra. Optional lion tracking (~$70; not budgeted).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, OPC campsite · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Dec 31 — North to Samburu + NYE by the river (Ol Pejeta → Samburu)

~2.5–3h via Nanyuki + Isiolo; drop into hot, dry north. Camp on the Ewaso Ng'iro river. PM drive. Quiet NYE under the stars.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu/Buffalo Springs campsite · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 01 — Samburu Special Five (Samburu National Reserve)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, Beisa oryx, Somali ostrich. Vulturine guineafowl, river elephants. Midday siesta (30–35°C).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sat Jan 02 — Leopards + river day (Samburu National Reserve)

Zero travel. Search riverine forest for leopard; lions, cheetah possible. Optional Samburu village visit (~$20–30 pp, paid locally).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Samburu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sun Jan 03 — Back to the cool highlands (Samburu → Nyahururu)

~3.5h via Isiolo + Nanyuki. Thomson's Falls (74 m) walk to the bottom. Camp at the falls; cool night (fleece).

*Overnight:* Thomson's Falls campsite, Nyahururu · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Mon Jan 04 — Short hop to Nakuru (Nyahururu → Lake Nakuru)

~1.5h downhill to Lake Nakuru NP. Camp inside the park. PM drive: rhino, Rothschild's giraffe.

*Overnight:* KWS campsite, Lake Nakuru NP · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Tue Jan 05 — Nakuru day (Lake Nakuru NP)

Zero travel. Dawn drive to Baboon Cliff. Black + white rhino, flamingos/pelicans (numbers vary), tree-climbing lions if lucky. Siesta.

*Overnight:* KWS campsite, Lake Nakuru NP · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Wed Jan 06 — West to the rainforest (Lake Nakuru → Kakamega Forest)

~4–4.5h via Eldoret. Rondo Retreat inside the forest, full board. Dusk walk: colobus calling.

*Overnight:* Rondo Retreat, Kakamega Forest · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Jan 07 — Rainforest day (Kakamega Forest)

Zero travel. Dawn bird walk with local guide (~Ksh300–500 pp): great blue turaco, hornbills. Black-and-white colobus, De Brazza's + red-tailed monkeys. Lirhanda Hill. Optional night walk (pottos, bushbabies).

*Overnight:* Rondo Retreat, Kakamega Forest · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 08 — Tea country (Kakamega → Kericho)

~2.5–3h via Kisumu. Tea-estate walk, factory visit. Easy afternoon.

*Overnight:* Budget hotel, Kericho · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 09 — Into the Mara conservancy (Kericho → Masai Mara)

~3–3.5h via Bomet + Mulot (last hour rough). Camp in Enonkishu (or Pardamat/Ol Chorro). Dusk drive.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Sun Jan 10 — Full Mara day (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Dawn + late drives, off-road, few vehicles. Big cats, plains game, newborn topi. Siesta.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Mon Jan 11 — Maasai walk + night drive (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Morning walk with Maasai ranger. PM drive. Night drive if allowed (aardvark, bat-eared fox, genets).

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Tue Jan 12 — Extra Mara day (Mara conservancy)

Zero travel. Full-day drive with picnic lunch, following whatever the guides found. Sundowner.

*Overnight:* Operator camp, Mara conservancy · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Wed Jan 13 — Road to Naivasha (Mara → Lake Naivasha)

~4–4.5h via Narok + Mai Mahiu. Lakeshore camp. Hippos at dusk.

*Overnight:* Lakeshore campsite, Naivasha · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Thu Jan 14 — Hell's Gate + boat (Lake Naivasha / Hell's Gate)

No long travel (30m local). Bike or walk Hell's Gate among zebra + giraffe, gorge hike. PM boat + Crescent Island walk. Rest.

*Overnight:* Lakeshore campsite, Naivasha · *Meals:* B, L, D

### Fri Jan 15 — Airport hotel (Naivasha → Nairobi (JKIA area))

~2.5–3h via A104 + Southern Bypass. Tip crew. Shower, repack, early night.

*Overnight:* Budget hotel near JKIA · *Meals:* B; L, D own

### Sat Jan 16 — Fly home (Nairobi → Istanbul → Los Angeles)

Wake ~02:45, 10–20m to JKIA. TK608 04:50 → IST 11:45. ~2h45 airside connect. TK9 ~14:30 → LAX ~18:15 same day. ~25h door to door.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Already have yellow fever shot** (−$200 pp): Skip the $200 vaccine. Bring your yellow card.
- **Skip Kakamega + Kericho; home Jan 13** (−$800 pp): Nakuru → Mara direct (~4.5–5h). Loses the rainforest primates + turacos; 3 days shorter.
- **Come home Jan 14 instead of Jan 16** (−$450 pp): Drop the extra Mara day + Hell's Gate day. Same loop otherwise.
- **Ol Pejeta specials: northern white rhino + lion tracking** (+$140 pp): Visit the last northern white rhino(s) with their keepers (~$70; check status before booking) + lion tracking with researchers (~$70).
- **Fly Mara → Wilson; Nairobi instead of Naivasha** (−$90 pp): ~2.5h door to door vs 4–4.5h drive. Drops Naivasha + Hell's Gate; 2 Nairobi hotel nights instead (Sheldrick, Giraffe Centre possible).
- **Fly Nairobi → Nanyuki for Ol Pejeta** (+$204 pp): ~2.5h door to door vs ~4h drive. Crew drives up empty anyway.
- **Tented-camp comfort at Ol Pejeta + Samburu** (+$650 pp): Sweetwaters Serena 2n + Samburu Riverside Camp 3n, full board; crew keeps vehicle + guide.

## Practical

## Morocco stopover
- **Visa (US passport):** US: Morocco visa-free 90 days (passport 6+ months, 1 blank page). CDG airside transit: no visa, no EES/ETIAS. Cairo airside: no visa. Kenya eTA must cover Dec 28 entry.
- **Safety — US State Dept Level 2: Exercise Increased Caution (terrorism). Advisory reissued Sep 4 2026, level unchanged.:** Stable, tourist-friendly; Level 2 for general terrorism (tourist sites, transport, markets; last deadly attacks 2011 Marrakech, 2018 Imlil). Daily risks: petty theft, medina hustlers, winter mountain roads. Merzouga near closed Algerian border, but dune zone safe.
  - Licensed driver, daylight only; Tizi n'Tichka (2,260 m), Midelt road may ice. Check night before.
  - Medina: ignore faux guides; agree henna/photo prices first; watch pickpockets.
  - Stay in Erg Chebbi tourist zone, not east toward Algeria; stick with camp guide at night.
  - Bottled/filtered water, few raw salads early; carry rehydration salts.
  - Desert and Azrou below 0 C at night: warm layer, hat, sleeping-bag liner.
  - Women may get medina catcalls; modest dress, firm 'la, shukran'.
  - Stay airside in Cairo; one ticket for whole routing. EgyptAir ~50-60% on time.
  - Enroll STEP; carry passport copy for rural police checkpoints.
- **Weather in late Dec:** Dry, sunny. Marrakech 18-20 C / 5-7 C. Ouarzazate/Dades 16-18 C / 0-3 C. Merzouga 17-20 C / 1-5 C (heavy blankets). Azrou/Ifrane (1,250-1,650 m) ~10 C, nights below freezing, snow possible. Tichka may close briefly after snow. Sunset ~17:45.

## Entry
- Kenya eTA $30, **etas.gov.ke only**. Türkiye: airside connections only, no visa needed. Passport 6+ mo, 2 blank pages.

## Do we need a rental car? No.
- Safari days: the camping operator's 4x4 + driver-guide is in the crew price. You never drive.
- Self-drive is possible (e.g. Roadtrip Africa Land Cruisers + rooftop tent), but **Mara conservancies don't allow self-drive vehicles**, you'd do every 4–5h drive yourselves on the left, and an 8-day self-drive package starts ~€1,730 pp: not cheaper.
- Nairobi: Uber/Bolt. Everything else in the crew vehicle.

## Road reality
- Longest days: Dec 29 (~4h), Jan 6 (4–4.5h), Jan 13 (4–4.5h). Rest day before each.
- Samburu → Nakuru and Kakamega → Mara are split with overnights (Thomson's Falls, Kericho).
- Kericho → Mara via Mulot: last hour rough; ask operator about rain.

## Weather
- Samburu hot + dry (30–35°C days). Kakamega: afternoon showers, muddy trails: rain jacket + closed shoes. Highlands cold at night.

## Health (CDC)
- Yellow fever recommended outside Nairobi. Malaria pills from Dec 27 to 7 days after leaving Kenya (Kakamega/west is higher risk). DEET.

## Camping
