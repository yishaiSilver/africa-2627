# Republic of Congo: Lésio-Louna gorillas and hippos, Conkouati-Douli turtles and chimps

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=congo

**Depart LAX:** 2026-12-26 · **Return LAX:** 2027-01-12 · **Planned cost:** $5,860 per person

## Overview

- **What this is:** Congo's version of Loango on a $6k budget. **Conkouati-Douli** is the coastal park just south of the Gabon border: lagoons, beaches with nesting turtles, coastal forest with elephants and chimps, the closest landscape to Loango. **Lésio-Louna**, north of Brazzaville, is savanna and gallery forest on the Louna and Léfini rivers, with hippos and gorillas.
- **Dates:** LAX Sat Dec 26 → LAX Tue Jan 12. 15 nights on the ground, 2 on planes. Same dates as the Tanzania and Kenya-minus-Kakamega trips.
- **Route:** Brazzaville 1n → **Lésio-Louna 4n** → Brazzaville 1n → ✈ Pointe-Noire 1n → **Conkouati-Douli 6n** → Tchimpounga → Pointe-Noire 2n → home.
- **Gorillas (a nice-to-have):** the ones you're likely to see are at Lésio-Louna, a population founded by released orphans (Aspinall Foundation) that now lives free and breeds in the wild; you see them from the boat. Conkouati has truly wild, unhabituated gorillas: signs are common, sightings are luck. Habituated or bai-watched wild gorillas mean Odzala, which doesn't fit $6k on these dates (see the options: Camp Imbalanga is closed Dec 16 – Jan 4, and Kamba runs ~$15k). The chimps are **released and orphaned** too (HELP Congo's on the Douli river, JGI's at Tchimpounga).
- **Wildlife:** hippos near-certain on the Louna; gorillas likely from the boat; forest elephants possible at Conkouati (tracks and dung are certain, sightings aren't); monkeys at both parks. **Herps** are the strong suit: leatherbacks and olive ridleys nesting (Nov–Feb), dwarf crocodiles, monitors, chameleons and frogs on night walks.
- **Diving / snorkelling:** **left out.** There's no dive operation to book on the Congo coast, and the Atlantic here is murky surf. The water time is boats on the Louna and the Conkouati lagoon, plus a swim in Lésio-Louna's clear Lac Bleu.
- **Budget: ~$5,860 pp** (≈ $11,720 for two). Flights are $2,100 of it. Lésio-Louna and Conkouati are costed from operator listings, not confirmed quotes; the $300 contingency covers that. $0 for yellow fever if your card is still valid (it's lifelong), which takes the total to ~$5,610.

## Itinerary

### Sat Dec 26 — Fly toward Brazzaville (Los Angeles → in flight)

Air France LAX → Paris overnight, connecting to Brazzaville (BZV). Book it as an open jaw: into Brazzaville, home from Pointe-Noire. Carry the yellow fever card in your hand luggage; it's checked on arrival.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* In-flight

### Sun Dec 27 — Land in Brazzaville (in flight → Brazzaville)

Land in the evening. Show the visa and yellow fever card; the hotel or the Lésio-Louna operator does the airport pickup. Brazzaville is UTC+1.

*Overnight:* Hotel in Brazzaville · *Meals:* In-flight; D hotel

### Mon Dec 28 — North to Lésio-Louna (Brazzaville → Lésio-Louna)

Operator's 4x4 north ~150 km (3–4h, the last stretch on sand tracks across the Batéké plateau) to the reserve camp at Iboubikro. Afternoon walk along the gallery forest; first evening on the Louna for hippos coming out to graze. In the rains the final track can be impassable after a storm; the operator decides on the day.

*Overnight:* Lésio-Louna camp (Iboubikro) · *Meals:* B hotel; L packed; D camp

### Tue Dec 29 — Boat to the gorillas (Lésio-Louna Gorilla Reserve)

Zero travel. Motorboat up the Louna with the reserve's keepers to the islands and banks where the released gorillas live; you watch from the boat. Hippos and crocodiles along the river. Afternoon swim in Lac Bleu, a clear blue lake in the reserve. Night walk around camp for frogs, geckos and nightjars.

*Overnight:* Lésio-Louna camp (Iboubikro) · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Wed Dec 30 — Léfini river and savanna (Lésio-Louna Gorilla Reserve)

Zero travel. Second boat trip, toward the Léfini side for more hippo pods and a second try for the gorillas. Savanna walk in the afternoon (monitor lizards, savanna birds, monkeys in the forest strips). Night walk with a torch along the river edge for crocodile eyeshine and frogs.

*Overnight:* Lésio-Louna camp (Iboubikro) · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Thu Dec 31 — Last boat morning, New Year's Eve in camp (Lésio-Louna Gorilla Reserve)

Zero travel. Dawn boat trip (best light, most hippo activity), a slow afternoon, a last swim at Lac Bleu. New Year's Eve in camp, away from Brazzaville's crowds.

*Overnight:* Lésio-Louna camp (Iboubikro) · *Meals:* B, L, D camp

### Fri Jan 01 — Back to Brazzaville (Lésio-Louna → Brazzaville)

Late morning drive back (~3–4h). Public holiday: the afternoon is for rest, or a taxi to the Djoué rapids on the Congo River at sunset. Night in Brazzaville because holiday flights are thin and the coast leg is easier from a morning flight.

*Overnight:* Hotel in Brazzaville · *Meals:* B camp; L, D own

### Sat Jan 02 — Fly to the coast (Brazzaville → Pointe-Noire)

Morning flight to Pointe-Noire (~1h; several local airlines fly it daily). Afternoon on the Côte Sauvage beach; meet the Conkouati operator to go over the plan (turtle walks, the Douli boat trip, food).

*Overnight:* Hotel in Pointe-Noire · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Sun Jan 03 — North up the coast to Conkouati (Pointe-Noire → Conkouati-Douli)

Operator's 4x4 north ~150 km (4–6h with ferry and river crossings) to Conkouati-Douli. Afternoon on the lagoon. After dark, the first beach walk with the turtle monitors: leatherbacks (up to ~2 m long) and olive ridleys come up to nest. Red lights only, and no flash.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D lodge

### Mon Jan 04 — Douli river: released chimps (Conkouati-Douli NP)

Zero travel. Boat up the lagoon and the Douli river into the area where HELP Congo has released orphaned chimps since the 1990s; they're seen from the boat. Monkeys along the banks. Night walk in the creeks for dwarf crocodiles and tree frogs.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B, L, D lodge

### Tue Jan 05 — Forest walk for elephants (Conkouati-Douli NP)

Zero travel. Early forest walk with a ranger on elephant trails: forest elephants and buffalo are possible, red-capped mangabeys and mandrills shy but present; monitor lizards, chameleons, butterflies. Turtle beach after dark.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B, L, D lodge

### Wed Jan 06 — Lagoon mouth: dolphins and birds (Conkouati-Douli NP)

Zero travel. Boat to the lagoon mouth, home to Atlantic humpback dolphins (one of the last populations), with terns, pelicans and ospreys. Afternoon off; rain is likely. Night walk.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B, L, D lodge

### Thu Jan 07 — Second Douli trip or savanna edge (Conkouati-Douli NP)

Zero travel. A second boat trip up the Douli (chimps and elephants show differently every day), or the coastal savanna for buffalo. Herp-focused night walk: ask the guide for chameleons, snakes and frogs at the forest edge. Turtle beach after.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B, L, D lodge

### Fri Jan 08 — Last Conkouati day (Conkouati-Douli NP)

Zero travel. A morning on whatever has worked best (a third boat trip or the forest), a slow afternoon, and a last turtle night. This day is also slack if rain cancelled an earlier boat trip.

*Overnight:* Eco-lodge, Conkouati-Douli · *Meals:* B, L, D lodge

### Sat Jan 09 — Tchimpounga chimp sanctuary on the way south (Conkouati → Tchimpounga → Pointe-Noire)

Drive back south. Stop at the Jane Goodall Institute's Tchimpounga sanctuary (~50 km north of Pointe-Noire) by prior arrangement: orphaned chimps in large forest enclosures and on the release islands of the Kouilou. JGI sets the visit rules and fee; book it ahead. Night in Pointe-Noire.

*Overnight:* Hotel in Pointe-Noire · *Meals:* B lodge; L, D own

### Sun Jan 10 — Diosso gorges and the Kouilou mangroves (Pointe-Noire)

A buffer if the Conkouati road ran late. Otherwise a taxi to the Gorges de Diosso, a red-rock canyon ~25 km north, and a pirogue on the lower Kouilou for mangroves, monitor lizards, kingfishers and possible crocodiles.

*Overnight:* Hotel in Pointe-Noire · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Mon Jan 11 — Beach morning, fly home (Pointe-Noire → in flight)

Côte Sauvage beach, late check-out, then Air France overnight to Paris.

*Overnight:* Plane · *Meals:* B hotel; L, D own

### Tue Jan 12 — Arrive home (in flight → Los Angeles)

Connect in Paris; arrive LAX the same afternoon.

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* In-flight

## Options & swaps

- **Odzala by road: Camp Imbalanga, 2 nights, wild gorillas at the bai (over budget)** (+$1,100 pp): African Parks' camp in Odzala: $400 pp/night full board plus $80/night in entry, conservation and community fees; Imbalanga bai visit included, Moba bai day $33, Mambili river boat $75 (hippos), gorilla-trail walks $25. Gorillas are wild and not habituated: watched at the bai, not tracked, and not guaranteed. Closed Dec 16 – Jan 4, so it only works Jan 5–7, and it's ~2 days by 4x4 each way from Brazzaville (~$700 pp for the vehicle and the Owando stopover nights). Replacing Lésio-Louna, it adds ~$1,100 pp net, to ~$6,950.
- **Odzala with Kamba: habituated gorillas at Ngaga (far over budget)** (+$15,600 pp): Kamba's 7-night Odzala Discovery (Ngaga gorillas ×2, Lango bai elephants, Mboko hippos), Mon Dec 28 – Mon Jan 4: $14,930 pp at 2026 rack, plus cancel-for-any-reason insurance, which Kamba requires. The only way to trek habituated gorillas in Congo. Brings the trip to ~$21,500 pp.
- **Nouabalé-Ndoki week instead of Lésio-Louna (over budget)** (+$5,500 pp): WCS-run northern park: wild gorillas and elephants at Mbeli Bai from a platform, gorilla tracking at Mondika. Flights to Ouesso plus a boat to Bomassa; rough and hard to book. Week-long trips are estimated at $6,000–8,000 pp on their own.
- **Fly home Jan 9 (drop the Pointe-Noire days)** (−$200 pp): Fly out the evening after Tchimpounga and land Jan 10. Loses the road buffer and the Diosso/Kouilou day.
- **Lésio-Louna 3 nights instead of 4** (−$100 pp): Fly to Pointe-Noire on Dec 31 instead; New Year's Eve on the coast and one more Pointe-Noire night.

## Practical

## Entry
- **Visa:** required in advance. US citizens get a 6-month multiple-entry tourist visa from the embassy in Washington, with an invitation letter or hotel booking. Allow 4+ weeks. The US entry ban on Congolese nationals (June 2025) is one-way: there's no reported Congolese suspension of visas for Americans, unlike Gabon and Chad. Confirm with the embassy when you apply.
- **Yellow fever certificate:** mandatory and checked on arrival.
- Passport 6+ months with blank pages.

## Packing
- **Headtorch each, with a red setting**, for night walks and turtle beaches (no white light or flash near nesting turtles).
- Rain jacket, quick-dry clothes, sandals for boats and wet landings, boots for forest walks.
- Binoculars and a long lens (the gorillas and chimps are watched from boats); dry bags.
- Snacks and water purification for Lésio-Louna and Conkouati; food in camp is basic.

## Money
- Central African CFA franc (XAF), pegged to the euro. Bring **euros in cash**; ATMs in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire only. Park fees, boats and tips are cash.

## Season
- The south of the country is in its rains from October to April, with a short drier spell around January. Expect heavy showers, and the occasional cancelled boat trip or blocked track; the spare Conkouati and Pointe-Noire days cover that.
- Hot and humid (~25–32 °C). Leatherback nesting runs Nov–Feb on the Conkouati beaches.

## Health and safety
- US advisory **Level 2** (Exercise Increased Caution) for crime; US staff need two vehicles for overland travel outside Brazzaville. Don't walk at night in Brazzaville or Pointe-Noire, and avoid demonstrations.
- The Brazzaville–Pointe-Noire road (RN1) has local blockades near Mindouli; this plan flies that leg.
- Ebola: no cases in the Republic of Congo as of mid-2026; outbreaks are in the neighbouring DRC. Check before you fly.
- Medical care is poor outside Brazzaville. Evacuation cover is essential.


## Open questions

1. Gorillas are a nice-to-have: are Lésio-Louna's free-living gorillas from the boat (plus a chance of wild ones at Conkouati) enough? Odzala's Camp Imbalanga, with wild gorillas at a bai, adds ~$1,100 pp net and only works after Jan 4.
2. ~$5,860 pp stays under $6k only if the flights come in near $2,100. If they don't, cut a Pointe-Noire day (−$200) or a Lésio-Louna night (−$100)?
3. Six nights at Conkouati is long for a basic lodge, but it means several turtle nights and room for rained-off boat trips. OK, or move a night to Pointe-Noire?
