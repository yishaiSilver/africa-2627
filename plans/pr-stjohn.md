# Old San Juan + St. John: Virgin Islands National Park

> Live, interactive version with feedback: https://yishaisilver.github.io/africa-2627/?trip=pr-stjohn

**Depart LAX:** 2026-11-07 · **Return LAX:** 2026-11-14 · **Planned cost:** $2,107 per person

## Overview

- **Dates:** LAX Sat Nov 7 → LAX **Sat Nov 14**. 2 nights Puerto Rico, 5 nights St. John (US Virgin Islands).
- **Route (1 real move):** Old San Juan 2n → fly SJU→STT Mon Nov 9 → ferry to Cruz Bay, St. John 5n → Sat Nov 14 ferry + flight STT→SJU, home ~17:00.
- **Flights:** LAX–SJU round trip ~$350, plus two separate STT↔SJU hops (~35–40m each).
- **National park:** ~60% of St. John is Virgin Islands National Park: Trunk Bay snorkel trail, Maho Bay turtles, Waterlemon Cay, Annaberg ruins, Reef Bay petroglyphs.
- **Slow days:** Sun Nov 8 (Old San Juan), Thu Nov 12 and Fri Nov 13 (St. John).
- **Getting around:** walk + safari taxis; jeep 1 day (Nov 11). Drive on the left.
- **Not here:** El Yunque, Vieques bio bay (St. John has none).
- **Pace:** longest travel day Mon Nov 9 (~4h door to door). ~$2,107 pp.

## Itinerary

### Sat Nov 07 — Fly to Puerto Rico (Los Angeles → San Juan)

Early 1-stop (via MIA/CLT/DFW/ATL/JFK). LAX ~06:00–08:00 → SJU ~18:00–21:00 (+4h clock). Taxi 20m to Old San Juan. Street food, sleep in tomorrow.

*Overnight:* Old San Juan – CasaBlanca Hotel · *Meals:* In-flight; D own

### Sun Nov 08 — Slow day in Old San Juan (Old San Juan)

Sleep in after the flight. Late breakfast. ~11:00 El Morro (one ~$10 fee covers San Cristóbal too), Paseo del Morro walk. Lunch, siesta. Late afternoon: San Cristóbal or just the blue streets, Paseo de la Princesa at sunset. No driving, no packing.

*Overnight:* Old San Juan – CasaBlanca Hotel · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Mon Nov 09 — Hop to St. John (San Juan → St. Thomas → St. John)

~08:30 taxi 20m to SJU. JetBlue or Cape Air ~10:00, ~35–40m to St. Thomas (STT). Taxi ~30–40m to Red Hook (per-person fares, + bags). Ferry on the hour, 20m to Cruz Bay. Lunch, check in. Late afternoon: Lind Point Trail (~1.1 mi) to Honeymoon Beach for a first swim. Sunset in Cruz Bay.

*Overnight:* Cruz Bay, St. John – St. John Inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Tue Nov 10 — Trunk Bay and Cinnamon Bay (St. John north shore)

Safari taxi ~08:30 to Trunk Bay ($5 pp): underwater snorkel trail with signs. Go early, before cruise-day crowds from St. Thomas. Taxi or walk on to Cinnamon Bay: sugar-mill ruins loop, easy snorkel off the point. Lunch at the beach. Back by mid-afternoon; siesta.

*Overnight:* Cruz Bay, St. John – St. John Inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Wed Nov 11 — Jeep day: turtles and the best reef (Maho Bay · Annaberg · Waterlemon Cay)

Pick up a jeep in Cruz Bay ~08:00 (drive on the LEFT). ~08:30 Maho Bay: shallow seagrass, sea turtles very likely. ~10:30 Annaberg sugar-mill ruins (free). Leinster Bay trail ~0.8 mi to Waterlemon Cay: best snorkel on the island (turtles, rays, starfish). Current on the far side of the cay: stay close in. Late lunch in Coral Bay. Back over Centerline Road overlooks. Return jeep.

*Overnight:* Cruz Bay, St. John – St. John Inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Thu Nov 12 — Slow day (St. John)

Sleep in. Beach of choice (Honeymoon or Salomon via Lind Point, or taxi back to Maho). Optional morning hike: Reef Bay Trail from Centerline Road, ~2.2 mi downhill to the Taíno petroglyphs and sugar-mill ruins, steep climb back (~3–4h; taxi to/from trailhead). Siesta. Sunset dinner in Cruz Bay.

*Overnight:* Cruz Bay, St. John – St. John Inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Fri Nov 13 — Last beach day, pack (St. John)

Easy morning swim (or Cinnamon/Maho again by safari taxi). Early dinner, pack. Early night: tomorrow starts ~06:30. Or take the isla-verde-last-night option: fly to SJU this afternoon and sleep by the airport.

*Overnight:* Cruz Bay, St. John – St. John Inn · *Meals:* B, L, D own

### Sat Nov 14 — Home via San Juan (St. John → St. Thomas → San Juan → Los Angeles)

07:00 ferry Cruz Bay → Red Hook (20m). Taxi ~35m to STT. Clear CBP (US customs/agriculture check before boarding; passport easiest). ~10:45–11:30 Cape Air or JetBlue to SJU (~35m), land ~noon. ~5h buffer. Lunch at the airport. ~17:00 flight home, 1 stop, lands LAX late night (−4h clock).

*Overnight:* Home · *Meals:* B, L own; D in flight

## Options & swaps

- **Last night by SJU instead of St. John** (−$32 pp): Fly STT → SJU Fri Nov 13 afternoon, sleep at Villa del Sol (Isla Verde, 10m to SJU). No ferry–taxi–customs–flight chain on departure day. Lose half a beach day.

- **Sleep in the park: Cinnamon Bay eco-tent** (+$25 pp): Swap St. John Inn for a beach eco-tent at Cinnamon Bay (no AC, shared bathhouse). Trunk/Maho close; Cruz Bay food by taxi.

## Practical

## Entry
- US citizens: no passport needed for PR or USVI. **But** flying USVI → PR/mainland you clear **CBP at STT** before boarding (USVI is outside US customs). Passport easiest; else REAL ID + certified birth certificate.
- Duty-free allowance in USVI ($1,600 pp) if shopping.

## Flights
- LAX–SJU round trip (~$350): ~1 stop each way. Return ~17:00 SJU lands LAX late night.
- SJU ↔ STT: JetBlue (A320, bags extra) or Cape Air (9-seat Cessna, ~12+/day each way, weight limits). ~35–40m.
- **Separate tickets:** the airline won't protect the SJU connection. Nov 14 plan gives ~5h buffer; Cape Air runs often enough to rebook. Bags: re-check at SJU.
- Ask: is a multi-city LAX→SJU, STT→LAX (AA via MIA/CLT) close to $350 + $120? That removes the Nov 14 hop.
- PR and USVI = AST (UTC−4). LA = PST after Nov 1: **+4h**.

## St. Thomas → St. John
- STT airport → Red Hook: open-air taxis, fixed per-person fares + bag fee. ~30–40m.
- Red Hook ↔ Cruz Bay passenger ferry: hourly on the hour, ~20m, $8.15 pp one way (non-resident) + ~$4/bag. Cash/card at window.
- Also Charlotte Amalie ↔ Cruz Bay ferry (~45m, fewer runs): closer to STT but longer boat.
- Car barge Red Hook ↔ Cruz Bay (~$65/car one way) only if renting on St. Thomas. Rent on St. John instead.

## On St. John
- Safari taxis (open trucks) from Cruz Bay dock: per-person fares to Trunk, Cinnamon, Maho. Fewer after ~16:00; ask driver for pickup time.
- Jeep: book ahead (small fleet). **Drive on the LEFT**, US-style cars. Steep, winding roads; donkeys and goats on road.
- Park entry free; Trunk Bay $5 pp. No lifeguards most beaches.
- ATMs in Cruz Bay only. Groceries: Starfish Market.

## Snorkel
- Best: Waterlemon Cay (current on far side), Maho (turtles, seagrass, very calm), Trunk Bay trail (crowded on cruise days), Cinnamon point, Honeymoon.
- Nov = north swell season: north shore can get waves; Salt Pond / Coral Bay side calmer.
- Reef-safe (mineral) sunscreen or rash guard. Don't touch coral or turtles.
- Water ~28°C: no wetsuit.

## Weather
- Highs ~29°C/84°F. Short showers. Hurricane season ends Nov 30: watch NHC; free-cancel bookings.

## Money
- USD. St. John ~30% pricier than PR (food, rooms). Tip 15–20%; taxi drivers ~$1–2/bag.

## Hotel notes
- USVI hotel room tax 12.5%. Small inns usually no resort fee.
- Rates are early snapshots; Nov is shoulder season. Take refundable rates; recheck ~2 weeks out.
