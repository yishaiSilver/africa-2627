# Counter-review: the fact-check of the $5.5k no-crew loop

What this checks: the fact-check (`no-crew-fixed`, $6,136 pp) of Andrew's no-crew loop (`no-crew` as first priced, $5,447 pp),
and the merged plan now in `no-crew` ($5,845 pp). Questions asked: which of the fact-check's added costs aren't needed,
and what else can be cut without changing the route, the Naboisho camp or the parks.

Result: the lean version is in [`no-crew-lean`](../plans/no-crew-lean.md) at **$5,579 pp** (−$266 vs `no-crew`).
Taking the one-night Ol Pejeta option brings it to about **$5,440**.

## Additional cuts (applied in `no-crew-lean`)

1. **Insurance: medical + evacuation plan instead of comprehensive (−$150).** Most of a comprehensive premium pays for
   trip-cancellation cover. Ordinary cancellation cover doesn't pay out if you cancel out of fear of Ebola; only
   cancel-for-any-reason does, and that costs more. A single-trip medical plan with ≥$250k evacuation (GeoBlue Voyager,
   IMG Patriot) costs roughly $60–120 for three weeks. Check that it covers injuries as a matatu or boda passenger.
   The trade-off: the prepaid Naboisho deposit isn't protected.
2. **AMREF Bronze, started Jan 1 (−$14).** See above.
3. **Matatu to Talek on the way into the Mara (−$65).** You need the camp to confirm a Talek pickup in writing.
4. **Topkapı without the Harem (−$25).**
5. **Contingency rescaled to 5% of the new total (−$12).**

## Optional cuts that change the trip

- **One night at Ol Pejeta instead of two (−$140).** Arrive Jan 1 by midday, do a 4h afternoon drive and the 06:30 bush walk
  on Jan 2, and leave within 24h. That's one $110 entry and one Stables night saved, and the spare night moves to Nakuru.
  The cost is less rhino time.
- Already in the plan: yellow fever −$200 if you're already vaccinated.

## Corrections to options already in the plan

- **"Shared game-drive vehicle at Ol Pejeta" (−$110) probably isn't available.** OPC's tariff lists shared vehicles only for
  night game drives and lion tracking. The option text now says so; don't count the saving.

## Checked and left alone

- Ol Pejeta $110 per 24h (2025/2026 tariff), Lake Nakuru $90 (KSh 11,660). Hell's Gate $50 is the new KWS rate. The court's
  October 2025 suspension of the fee rise has no reported outcome, but parks are charging the new rates.
- Yellow fever ($200): CDC recommends it for this route.
- The Nairobi room held from Dec 30 for the 03:05 landing, and the JKIA hotel before the 04:50 flight. Both are needed.
