## Overview

## The trip

You spend **New Year's Eve in Istanbul**. Then you do a **private camping safari** with a Kenyan ground operator: 2 nights inside **Ol Pejeta Conservancy** (Laikipia), 1 night at Lake Naivasha, and 4 nights camping in a **Masai Mara private/community conservancy**. Next come 3 easy nights in Nairobi (Sheldrick baby elephants, Giraffe Centre, Karen). You finish with **2 free nights in Istanbul** on the way home.

One Turkish Airlines multi-city ticket covers every international flight: LAX → Istanbul (5-night stop) → Nairobi → Istanbul (2-night stop) → LAX.

- **Depart LAX:** Sat **Dec 26, 2026**. That is the Saturday of Christmas week, so you have Christmas at home.
- **Return to LAX:** Thu **Jan 14, 2027**. That is "slightly earlier" than Jan 16, as you asked. Fri Jan 15 is an easy option.
- **Length:** 20 calendar days. 7 nights in Istanbul (5 + 2) and 10 nights in Kenya (7 in conservancies and on safari, 3 in Nairobi).

## Why this routing
1. **It avoids Kenya's festive prices.** Camps charge their top rates from Dec 20 to Jan 2, plus Christmas and New Year supplements (Porini adds $100 pppn on Dec 24–26, 31 and Jan 1). With Istanbul over Christmas week and New Year, every Kenya night is in January.
2. **Istanbul is the cheapest place to spend a day.** It costs about $90–130 per person per day, against $250+ on safari. The Turkish Airlines Stopover program gives US-origin economy tickets **2 free 4-star hotel nights**, for a stop of 20 hours to 7 days, on one leg only. Here it is used on the **return** in mid-January, when it is low season. So the New Year's Eve stay is one hotel for 5 nights with **no hotel change**.
3. **Naivasha splits the drive.** Ol Pejeta → Mara is 7–8 hours in one go. Via Lake Naivasha it becomes about 3.5–4h plus about 4.5–5h, with a boat ride and a walk among giraffe on Crescent Island in between. That replaces the Nanyuki → Mara bush flight (about $400 pp). Flights are an add-on if you would rather fly (see Alternatives).
4. **There is no 15kg bush-flight bag limit**, because the main plan is all road. Your Istanbul winter clothes ride along or stay with the operator.

## Ol Pejeta vs. the Masai Mara
**Ol Pejeta Conservancy is not in the Masai Mara.**
- **Where it is:** Laikipia, under Mt Kenya near Nanyuki. That is about 200 km north of Nairobi and about 250 km from the Mara.
- **What it's famous for:**
  - East Africa's largest black rhino sanctuary
  - the last two northern white rhinos (an unverified May 2026 report says Fatu died; check OPC news)
  - the Sweetwaters chimpanzee sanctuary
  - lion tracking
- **The Mara has its own private and community conservancies:** Naboisho, Olare Motorogi, Ol Kinyei, Mara North, Enonkishu, Olderkesi, Mara Siana and Pardamat. They offer:
  - few vehicles
  - off-road driving
  - walks with Maasai guides
  - night drives
- **This plan includes both,** because together they are Kenya's classic pairing.

## Practical

## Entry and documents
- **Türkiye:** no visa or e-visa for US citizens (90 days in any 180).
- **Kenya:** you need an **eTA** ($30), from **etas.gov.ke only**. Apply about 3 weeks out; processing takes about 3 working days.
- **Passport:** valid for 6 or more months after the trip, with 2 blank pages.

## Turkish Airlines Stopover rules
- **Stopover length:** 20 hours to 7 days. Both stops fit: about 5 days going out and about 2 days coming home.
- **Who qualifies:** the ticket number must start with 235, and it must be a round trip in one booking. US-origin economy gets **2 free 4-star nights**, on **one** leg only. N and R fare classes are reported as excluded.
- **How to request it:** after ticketing, and at least 72h before travel. The hotel is assigned.
- **Short layovers:** Touristanbul (6–24h) is the free fallback.

## Health (CDC)
- **Yellow fever:** recommended for Laikipia and the Mara, but not required arriving from the US or Türkiye. Book a clinic early.
- **Malaria:** present in Nairobi and all safari areas. Take atovaquone-proguanil from Jan 1 until 7 days after you leave Kenya. Use DEET or picaridin, and long sleeves at dusk.
- **Other vaccines:** routine shots, Hep A, typhoid and Tdap.
- **Water:** drink bottled or filtered water. Your camp cook supplies it.

## Camping-safari expectations
- **The tents:** walk-in canvas tents with camp beds, mattresses and bedding. The cook prepares 3 hot meals a day.
- **Washing:** bucket hot showers and a toilet tent (or campsite ablutions).
- **Your vehicle:** your own 4x4 with a pop-up roof, so there is no fixed drive schedule.
- **Safety:** a conservancy askari (guard) watches camp at night. Never walk outside the tent after dark without an escort.
- **Packing for camp:** bring a headlamp, a power bank, and a fleece and beanie (Laikipia nights are about 8–12°C).
- **Ask the operator:** how charging works and whether they supply sleeping bags.

## Insurance and evacuation
- Buy a comprehensive policy with **at least $250k medical evacuation**. It must cover road safaris, and bush flights if you add them.
- Add **AMREF Maisha Bronze ($10)** for air evacuation within Kenya.
- Cancellation cover matters because operator and camp balances are usually nonrefundable inside 30–60 days.

## Packing
- **Istanbul (Dec–Jan):** highs about 9°C / 48°F, lows about 3°C, frequent rain and occasional snow. Bring a warm waterproof jacket, a scarf and waterproof shoes.
- **Mosques:** women need a headscarf; everyone needs covered shoulders and knees.
- **Safari:**
  - neutral colors (no camouflage)
  - layers, sun hat, sunscreen
  - binoculars
  - UK Type G plug adapter
- **Bags:** soft duffels if you might add a bush flight, which has a 15kg limit.
- **Plastic bags are banned in Kenya.** Leave them at home.

## Tipping (per person, in USD)
- Driver-guide: $10–15 per day
- Camp cook: $5–8 per day
- Campsite or ranger: $5
- Transfer drivers: $5–10
- Istanbul restaurants: about 10%; hammam attendant 10–15%

## Money
- **Kenya:** about 130 KES per $1.
  - Park and conservancy gates are cashless: card or M-Pesa. M-Pesa comes with a Safaricom SIM, which needs your passport to register.
  - Carry small, crisp post-2013 US dollar bills for tips.
- **Türkiye:** about 48 TRY per $1. Cards are widely accepted. Prices in lira tend to rise.

## Safety
- **Kenya is at Level 2, Exercise Increased Caution** (Jul 2026).
  - Use Uber, Bolt or guesthouse cars in Nairobi and don't walk at night.
  - Avoid the Level 4 areas: the Somalia-border counties, Lamu and Tana River.
  - The conservancies are very safe.
- **Long road days (4–6h):** your operator's driver drives. Avoid driving after dark.
- **Istanbul on NYE:** expect heavy crowds and watch for pickpockets.

## Weather (early Jan, short dry season)
- **Laikipia and the Mara:** 25–28°C by day, 8–13°C at dawn. Occasional afternoon showers; green landscapes.
- **Naivasha:** mild.
- **Nairobi:** 18–24°C.

## Fees to re-check before you go
- **KWS park fees:** the increases to $80/$90 have been suspended by the courts more than once. This plan only touches KWS fees at the optional Nairobi National Park.
- **OPC tariff:** may rise for 2027.
- **Northern white rhino visit:** check OPC news before booking, because of the unverified 2026 report about Fatu.

## Open questions

1. Is the $5,000–$6,000 target per person (assumed), or for both of you together? If it's the total, the trip needs a very different design.
2. Are you happy with private serviced camping (walk-in tents with beds and your own cook) at Ol Pejeta and in the Mara as the main plan? Or would you add comfort: Serena at Ol Pejeta (+$340), Dorobo in Naboisho (+$800), or full lodge + fly-in with Porini (+$1,850)?
3. Istanbul: does 5 nights over New Year's Eve plus 2 free nights on the way home feel right? Or would you rather shorten Istanbul and add Kenya days (each extra safari day is about $250–350)?
4. Is returning Thu Jan 14 right, or would you prefer Fri Jan 15 (one more day, about +$150)?
5. The end of Kenya: 3 relaxed nights in Nairobi (Sheldrick, Giraffe Centre, Karen), or 3 nights at Diani Beach (+$380)?
6. Are you comfortable with 4–6 hour road days (Ol Pejeta → Naivasha → Mara → Nairobi)? Or would you pay +$290–690 to fly some legs?
7. Are any of these musts: a balloon flight (about $450–550), Ol Pejeta lion tracking or the northern white rhino visit (about $70 each), a Maasai village visit, or a Nairobi National Park drive?
8. Do you already have yellow fever vaccinations, and are your passports valid to at least July 2027?
